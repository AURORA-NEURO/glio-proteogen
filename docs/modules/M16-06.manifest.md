M16-06 records reviewer-owned discrepancy state beneath the KINOPHOS object
consumer. It preserves each source disagreement and reviewer decision without
mutating upstream evidence or relabeling another module's output. Critical,
novel/OOD, support-override, claim-promotion, release-exception, and unresolved
biological conflicts remain human-review obligations.

The queue rejects withheld or unresolved controls before traversal, requires
blinded assignments and append-only audit history, and abstains when assignment
coverage or resolution is incomplete. It does not emit kinase state, generic
all-omics fusion, treatment recommendations, identity/consent inference, or
unsupported-negative claims. `emits_parent=false` keeps the result contextual.

Executable requirement links are in
`docs/traceability/GLIO-PROTEOGEN-M16-06.csv`. The scenario corpus is synthetic,
non-clinical, and locked at `tests/fixtures/m16_06/scenarios.json`.

## Reviewer sign-off

M05-04 cannot self-approve. A governed release record must name an authenticated reviewer, role,
decision, timestamp, module/version, source commit, schema digests, fixture digest, 72-case eval,
focused tests/coverage, interface evidence, 25-call benchmark, recursive privacy/authority evidence,
and reviewed request/result/policy/configuration digests. CI assembles candidate evidence only.

## Rollback and recovery

1. Stop publication or reject/quarantine/abstain without overwriting inputs, outputs, or evidence.
2. Retain the exact request, upstream result, policy, ledger, result, digests, and review record.
3. Restore configuration or inputs through the governed external source; never repair or relabel
   them inside M05-04.
4. Submit a corrected request naming the superseded result digest and publish a new immutable
   result.
5. Replay schemas, 72 cases, focused tests, coverage, interfaces, benchmark, package, traceability,
   privacy/authority checks, and external review.

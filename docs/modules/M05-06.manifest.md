## Runtime and safe failure

The public builder accepts the complete M05-05 result envelope and verifies its
disposition, request/result binding, version, identity, support, provenance,
and seven authorization controls before reading aggregate observations. A
quarantined upstream result remains quarantined; an abstained or unsupported
upstream result remains abstained. Missing, unresolved, stale, forged, or
unsupported information never becomes a negative finding.

Accepted work executes eight deterministic normalization stages over seven
upstream detector classes and eight support factors. It returns typed analysis,
support ledger, transformation manifest, computation receipt, findings,
uncertainty, provenance, evidence, limitations, and a closed result envelope.
The implementation has no model weights, external traversal, mutable event
store, identity inference, modification localization, kinase inference,
all-omics fusion, treatment recommendation, or upstream mutation authority.

## Data, evidence, and benchmark manifest

The evaluator constructs a genuine M05-05 replay through the public builder and
then exercises accepted, quarantined, and abstained scenarios. The benchmark
builds and warms outside timing, then times exactly 25 public calls at the
`harmonize_ptm_localization_analysis` boundary and checks byte/digest/result
determinism. The observed local benchmark was 25/25 deterministic calls with
mean 2,701,240,716 ns, p50 2,656,482,300 ns, p95 2,956,385,200 ns, and maximum
3,002,191,000 ns. Request/result sizes were 156,594/175,213 bytes. These are
software-regression measurements, not scientific performance or clinical
claims.

## Reviewer, rollback, and claims ceiling

No module self-approves. A release record must name an authenticated reviewer,
role, decision, timestamp, reviewed commit, schema/evaluator/benchmark
digests, upstream digest, and owner confirmation. On discrepancy, stale
binding, policy failure, or unsupported state, preserve the immutable request
and evidence, quarantine or abstain, correct only governed source data, and
submit a new superseding request; never mutate or silently promote a prior
result. This module claims only deterministic aggregate harmonization under
the reviewed draft contract. It does not claim assay truth, PTM localization,
identity, consent, calibration, coverage, biological effect, treatment value,
or future robustness gates.

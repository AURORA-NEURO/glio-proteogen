## Data manifest

Only synthetic metadata are installed: opaque identifiers, enums, semantic versions, timestamps,
content digests, exact upstream result envelopes, role declarations, categorical evidence states,
and a derivation DAG. The builder contains no patient names, production identifiers, spectra,
peptide or protein sequences, accessions, variants, abundance values, copy number, clinical labels,
or treatment-history content. The output repeats no raw artifact content.

## Reviewer sign-off

Critical discrepancy, novel/OOD state, support override, claim promotion, release exception, and
unresolved biological conflict require an external governed reviewer. Release evidence must bind
the reviewer identity and role, decision, time, exact source revision, nine schema IDs, locked
fixture digest, evaluation report digest, benchmark report digest, wheel digest, and this manifest
digest. M05-02 cannot authenticate reviewers, approve itself, promote a claim, or confer clinical
authority.

## Rollback and recovery

1. Reject unauthorized or malformed ingress before governed artifact traversal.
2. Abstain on unresolved identity, unsupported configuration, or non-evaluable evidence; quarantine
   upstream nonconformance, swaps, collisions, cross-patient links, and producer drift.
3. Retain the immutable request, result, upstream envelopes, graph, findings, evidence, and receipt.
4. Disable the candidate deployment externally and restore the last approved artifact.
5. Correct evidence only under its owning upstream authority; never rewrite the prior result.
6. Submit a new request with `supersedes_result_digest` bound to the prior result.
7. Replay all 70 cases, exact 25-call benchmark, static/coverage/package gates, and interfaces.
8. Obtain new external sign-off bound to replacement digests before promotion.

## Claims ceiling

This manifest supports deterministic identity-lineage reconciliation only. It does not qualify
identity truth, PTM-localization accuracy, variant-peptide truth, biological consequence,
proteogenomic state, proteotype, subtype, kinase activity, copy-number regression, treatment
utility, nominal calibrated coverage, population transport, or clinical use.

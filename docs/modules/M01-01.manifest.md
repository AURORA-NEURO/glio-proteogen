## Owned responsibilities

M01-01 owns strict protocol registration, metadata conformance, compatibility evaluation,
explicit unresolved-value handling, typed upstream authorization gates, canonical
identity-key lineage binding, and deterministic audit evidence. It can say whether a document
is conformant with a registered protocol. It cannot authenticate the external decision issuer
or interpret the document as a biological or therapeutic conclusion.

The unit catalog is a closed subset of the official [UCUM 2.2 specification](https://ucum.org/ucum),
used under the published [UCUM license](https://ucum.org/license). Its version, dimensions,
and affine Decimal transforms are embedded for deterministic validation; the module performs
no network lookup at runtime.

## Prohibited responsibilities and outputs

- Kinase-state ownership or inference.
- Generic all-omics fusion.
- Treatment recommendations.
- Mutation of upstream identity, consent, quality, provenance, support, configuration, or
  intended-use objects.
- Inference of identity or consent.
- Erasure of disagreement or counter-evidence.
- Conversion of absent, unknown, redacted, unsupported, or not-applicable evidence into a
  negative finding.

## Evidence-weighted calibration model

M06-07 consumes the validated M06-06 decomposition as a selective model, not
as a value pass-through. The seven uncertainty dimensions are combined by an
explicit weighted risk functional (measurement 0.24, identification 0.16,
support 0.16, transport 0.14, model form 0.12, parameter 0.10, sampling
0.08). Support is `1 - risk`; calibration error adds a bounded 0.25 risk
penalty to the locked stratum error. Out-of-domain risk combines the M06-06
transport component with feature-level coverage of the GBM marker families
EGFR, PDGFRA, MET, CDK4, MDM2, MYCN, CDKN2A/B, PTEN, NF1, and chr10. A result
is released only when every estimate remains above the support threshold,
inside the OOD threshold, and below the calibration-error threshold. Missing
or non-estimable dimensions fail closed; they are never treated as zero risk.

## Responsibility and boundary

M06-05 integrates caller-declared biological, ontology, graph, topology,
conservation, chemistry, assay-physics, and disease constraints beneath the
Protein abundance model. It emits only a constraint-aware estimate and
satisfaction report for the parent biomarker-panel target.

The implementation does not own KINOPHOS kinase state, perform generic
all-omics fusion, recommend treatment, mutate upstream evidence, infer identity
or consent, erase disagreement, or turn missing/unsupported evidence into a
negative finding.

## Provisional interface

The request, constraint, evaluation, ablation, estimate, result, and replay
models live under `src/glio_proteogen/contracts/m06_05`. All ABI names,
limits, media types, and the numeric-comparison expression subset are review
scaffolding pending authority. Ingress is bounded and duplicate-key-safe; the
runtime never calls `eval` or traverses external content.

Hard constraints must be satisfied for integration. Soft constraints expose
with/without ablation effects. Unknown expressions, non-observed features, hard
violations, and unsupported support states abstain explicitly.

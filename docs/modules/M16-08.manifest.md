## Rollback and recovery

Critical discrepancy or policy violation produces `rollback`; other declared drift produces
`suspend`; healthy signals produce `continue`; missing, unsupported, or not-evaluable signals
produce `abstain` with human review. Inputs and outputs are content-digested and replayable.
Recovery is append-only: retain the immutable request/result, correct the upstream/configuration
source externally, submit a new request with `supersedes_result_digest`, and replay the complete
fixture, interface, and tamper suite. No result is overwritten or silently promoted.

## Executable evidence

`tests/fixtures/m18_06/scenarios.json` locks eight synthetic cases: resolved record,
critical escalation, missing assignment, non-evaluable history, control denial, upstream
media rejection, replay tamper, and duplicate discrepancy IDs. `evals/m18_06/run.py`
executes all eight with six adversarial cases and reports 100% adversarial pass coverage
(above the 95% target). `tests/integration/test_m18_06_interfaces.py` verifies strict schema,
API, CLI, service, engine, plugin, and replay parity. `evals/m18_06/benchmark.py` times one
warm-up plus 25 deterministic two-entry public calls with 500 ms mean and 750 ms p95 budgets.

All fixtures are synthetic and non-clinical. The queue records caller-declared review state;
it does not infer biological truth, authenticate reviewer authority, or approve a parent panel.
Unresolved, unsupported, or non-evaluable material remains visible through typed findings and
explicit review/abstention state.

## Review, rollback, and release

M18-06 cannot self-approve. A governed release record must bind the dossier SHA and slice,
schema digests, fixture/eval/benchmark digests, focused test result, source commit, and an
externally authenticated reviewer. Corrections supersede immutable records by new request and
result digests; no queue history, assignment, evidence, or prior output is overwritten,
relabelled, deduplicated, or silently promoted.

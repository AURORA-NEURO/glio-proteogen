## Responsibility and boundary

M26-07 records caller-declared change classification, revalidation gates,
champion/challenger shadow comparisons, staged rollout approval, and a tested
rollback point. It is an operational control record, not a biological model
and not an authenticated authorization service. It never infers identity,
consent, treatment, kinase activity, or all-omics conclusions.

The deterministic runtime performs complete request validation, seven-control
preflight, cross-reference closure, and canonical replay. `source_artifacts`
must retain every nested proposal, revalidation, comparison, and rollback
evidence reference, including the rollback restore artifact, with the exact
artifact ID, version, digest, and media type. A complete request produces a
staged package only when required revalidations pass, every shadow comparison
declares no regression, and tested rollback evidence is present.
Failed or unresolved material is retained in findings and returns an explicit
review-required abstention without a package.

## Safety and non-authority guarantees

- caller-declared artifacts and failed revalidations are preserved as evidence;
  abstention is never converted into negative evidence;
- accepted configuration, resolved identity/lineage, provenance, consent,
  quality, support, and intended-use controls are required before traversal;
- proposal IDs bind every revalidation and comparison; critical regressions,
  missing gates, duplicate JSON keys, forged tokens, tampered digests, and
  unsafe CLI overwrites are rejected;
- result IDs, request/result digests, seven uncertainty dimensions,
  provenance, evidence, limitations, and human-review requirements are closed
  and replay-verifiable;
- the module emits no kinase activity, generic all-omics fusion, treatment
  recommendation, identity inference, or consent inference.

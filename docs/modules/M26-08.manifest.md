## Responsibility and boundary

M26-08 records caller-declared retirement criteria, dependency migration,
evidence preservation, communication, and long-term archival. It is a
deterministic operational record and knowledge-transfer boundary, not a
biological model or authenticated authority service. It never infers
identity, consent, treatment, kinase activity, or generic all-omics results.

The runtime produces an executed retirement package only when all required
criteria are satisfied, every dependency migration is complete and changes
its reference, preserved evidence remains retrievable, communications are
acknowledged, the archive is verified, and no active dependencies remain.
Any failed, missing, unsupported, or unauthorized material is retained in
findings and produces a review-required abstention without a package.

## Safety and replay guarantees

- source artifacts, criteria, migrations, evidence, communications, and
  archive manifests remain caller-declared and provenance-bound;
- seven-control execution preflight fails closed and does not authenticate
  issuer identity or infer consent from payload content;
- strict duplicate-key JSON parsing, schema validation, API/SDK/CLI/plugin
  parity, and no-overwrite CLI behavior share one service boundary;
- request/result IDs and canonical digests reject altered content, forged
  tokens, cross-instance plugin tokens, and validly rehashed result-ID edits;
- seven uncertainty dimensions, support status, limitations, evidence, and
  human-review requirements are explicit in every result;
- abstention is not negative evidence and never emits a retirement package.

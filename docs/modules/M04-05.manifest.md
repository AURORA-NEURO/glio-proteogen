## Reviewer sign-off

M04-05 cannot self-approve. A release record must name an externally authenticated reviewer,
role, decision, timestamp, exact version/source commit, 13 schema digests, fixture/eval/interface/
benchmark digests, privacy and authority evidence, and reviewed upstream/policy/configuration
digests. Critical discrepancy, OOD state, support override, claim promotion, exception,
quarantine, abstention, or unresolved biological conflict remains external review work.

## Rollback and recovery

1. Reject, quarantine, or abstain without changing upstream or prior results.
2. Retain the exact request, upstream result, policy, profile, ledger/binding, derived result, and
   external review record.
3. Correct configuration or aggregate declarations only in the governed source; never repair,
   relabel, clip, impute, deduplicate, or reinterpret them inside M04-05.
4. Submit a new request naming the superseded immutable result digest and publish to a new path.
5. Replay the 15-case eval, 87 focused tests, 13 schemas, max totality, 25-call benchmark,
   privacy/authority controls, traceability, and external review.

Supersession is append-only and never deletes, overwrites, mutates, re-signs, or silently promotes
a prior result or evidence artifact.

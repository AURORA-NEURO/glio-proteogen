All executable data and fixture inputs are synthetic and non-clinical. The runtime does not open
artifact references, call a registry, persist model state, train weights, or infer identity,
consent, provenance, or support. Non-numeric, missing, unsupported-family, unknown-optimizer,
tampered, or denied-control requests fail closed through typed abstention or rejection.

The schema registry marks `provisionalAbi=true` and `pendingOwnerConfirmation=true`. No release
authority is implied by this local-only checkpoint. The coupled program lane requires at least
four observed marker proteins spanning two programs, emits deterministic 90% research intervals,
and abstains on insufficient or non-observed support without converting missingness into a
negative observation.
Marker identity matching is token-exact and canonicalizes common HGNC compound
spellings such as `MKI-67`/`MKI_67` and `HIF-1A`; substring-only identifiers do
not receive GBM program credit.

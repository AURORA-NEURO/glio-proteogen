## Responsibility and boundary

M26-04 exposes a typed, versioned, auditable access surface over caller-
declared gateway operations. The contract covers operation metadata,
authorization decisions, idempotency records, asynchronous jobs, compatibility
rules, typed errors, audit events, and a locked gateway configuration. It does
not authenticate issuer authority or infer permissions from payload content.

The runtime is stateless and deterministic. It validates the complete request,
performs seven-control preflight (configuration, identity/lineage, provenance,
consent, quality, support, and intended use), then either publishes a complete
surface or abstains without exposing it. API, SDK, CLI, and strict JSON plugin
paths share the same canonical service boundary and replay verifier.

## Safety and non-authority guarantees

- caller-declared source artifacts are retained and never relabeled, erased, or
  converted from missing to negative evidence;
- denied, unresolved, queued, incompatible, migration-required, or unsupported
  material is retained in findings and abstains fail closed;
- result IDs, request digests, payload digests, provenance, evidence,
  limitations, and all seven uncertainty dimensions are deterministic and
  replay-verifiable;
- duplicate JSON keys, non-object envelopes, unknown operation references,
  duplicate source IDs, forged tokens, tampered results, and unsafe output
  overwrites are rejected or sanitized;
- the module emits no kinase activity, generic all-omics fusion, treatment
  recommendation, identity inference, or consent inference.

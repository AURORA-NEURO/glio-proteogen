## Owned responsibilities

M01-02 owns deterministic reconciliation of patient, specimen, aliquot, section, analyte,
run, and derived-object identity and lineage. Only an explicit, authority-bound `same_as`
assertion can create an identity component. Matching tokens, concordance, labels, graph
proximity, and content digests cannot authorize a union. Pooling and demultiplexing use
separate, closed operations and preserve multi-subject ambiguity unless complete authoritative
channel evidence is present.

The module records exactly seven role-bound control evidence references. Provenance binds their
decisions and digests, the active configuration, and a digest of the complete request evidence
manifest. Each finding records a complete causal-reference count, bounded causal artifact
representatives, and a digest of its complete causal declaration set in the append-only ledger.
The module does not hold tokenization secrets, authenticate caller-declared authority artifacts,
or persist raw identifiers, genotypes, peptide measurements, or demultiplex tags.

## Prohibited responsibilities and outputs

- Direct identity, raw identifiers, genotype/allele/peptide material, ancestry, sex, or kinship.
- Identity union inferred from tokens, concordance, labels, proximity, or a shared digest.
- Mutation of upstream labels, identity assertions, consent, configuration, quality, support,
  provenance, or intended-use records.
- Erasure of disagreement or conversion of missing/unsupported evidence to a negative finding.
- Kinase-state inference, generic all-omics fusion, or treatment recommendations.
- Clinical or biological interpretation of an identity-lineage resolution.

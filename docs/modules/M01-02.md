# M01-02: sample identity and lineage resolution

M01-02 is the immutable identity boundary for patient, specimen, aliquot, section, analyte,
run, and derived-object records. It checks declared lineage, identifier assertions, and
privacy-minimized concordance evidence; it emits only an identity-resolution object and its
lineage graph. It never edits upstream labels, guesses a missing identity, collapses
disagreement, or recommends an analytical or clinical action. All locked fixtures are
synthetic and non-clinical.

## Internal authority and algorithm

The versioned M01-02 contract is the sole authority for identity semantics. External standards
are import/export adapters and vocabulary sources; none may weaken the closed node kinds,
transition table, cardinalities, subject-binding rules, or quarantine policy.

Resolution is deterministic and fail closed:

1. Strict contracts reject coercion, unknown properties, ambiguous identifiers, non-finite
   numbers, invalid Unicode boundary forms, and every collection above its declared cap.
2. Nodes and edges are keyed by scoped opaque identifiers. A token is meaningful only with its
   issuer/scope and declared node kind. A repeated logical lineage declaration cannot create a
   second producer and is surfaced for review; conflicting token reuse is a collision.
3. The ordinary-lineage transition table is closed. Pool and demultiplex relations are separate
   policy-gated operations, not special spellings of an ordinary edge.
4. Only explicit, authorized `same_as` assertions enter identity components. Matching tokens,
   similar labels, concordance evidence, or a shared content digest never create a union.
5. Authorized identity assertions are processed in canonical order with a disjoint-set union.
   A `different_from` contradiction, cross-kind assertion, scoped-token collision, or multiple
   patient anchors poisons the whole connected component atomically. A poisoned bridge cannot
   leak two apparently resolved subcomponents.
6. Ordinary lineage is checked iteratively using Kahn topological traversal. Cycles, forbidden
   transitions, over-depth graphs, and cross-patient ordinary derivations quarantine all affected
   material. A material target has one distinct producer; only a `derived_object` may have
   multiple producers, and every such producer must be `computed_from`. The implementation
   performs neither recursive graph walking nor all-pairs search.
7. Pools preserve an explicit multi-patient state. `pooled_from` is permitted only for the
   contract-declared same-kind inputs and only when enabled. Demultiplexing requires unique,
   complete, authoritative channels, but a channel tag never becomes a patient identity; a
   narrower target identity must come from an independent authority-bound membership assertion.
8. Concordance observations are deduplicated by root observation, the complete content-evidence
   digest set, unordered comparison endpoints, method and version, assay lineage, panel,
   reference, comparison target, and policy identity. Support, counter-evidence, ambiguous,
   missing, and excluded observations remain separate. Concordance can corroborate, downgrade,
   quarantine, or abstain; it can never merge nodes or promote an unresolved identity.
9. Findings and output collections are deterministically sorted. Canonical digests are invariant
   to semantically unordered collection order and process hash seed, while duplicate concordance
   declarations cannot inflate aggregates. No internal union-find representative is exposed as a
   biological identity.
10. Every resolution retains the seven uncertainty dimensions, support, provenance, evidence,
    and limitations required by the dossier. Critical, out-of-distribution, override, release,
    and unresolved-conflict decisions require governed review.

Let `V` be nodes, `E` lineage edges, `A` identity assertions, and `C` concordance observations.
Indexing, union-find, and topological checks are `O(V + E + A alpha(V) + C)` before canonical
sorting, which adds `O((V + E + A + C) log(V + E + A + C))`. Auxiliary memory is linear. The
request caps are 10,000 nodes, 40,000 lineage edges, 20,000 assertions, 50,000 concordance
observations, and 50,000 evidence items. Each observation may contribute at most 10,000,000
informative loci, so every aggregate locus field is capped at 500,000,000,000. Further caps are
256 nodes per identity component, 64 retained evidence references per bounded declaration or
finding, a lineage-depth policy ceiling of 64, 1,000 reported issues including the overflow
marker, and a 4 MiB transport ceiling.

## Safe-failure and privacy semantics

Typed findings distinguish identity, lineage, pooling, demultiplexing, concordance, and
authorization failures. These are findings, not relabel commands. The only operational
responses are retain, quarantine, or human review; no result mutates an upstream record.

Genetic and proteomic fingerprints are privacy-minimized, not anonymous. Raw reads, genotypes,
allele counts, variant-peptide intensities, reusable cross-project sketches, direct patient
identifiers, and inferred ancestry, sex, disease, or kinship are forbidden from the resolution
output. Comparisons are restricted to the supplied authorized scope. Each persisted finding
records its complete causal-reference count, at most 64 bounded content-addressed representatives,
and a digest over the complete causal declaration set. Provenance separately binds the complete
request evidence manifest and includes that digest among its inputs. Neither structure retains
raw measurements, tokens, or demultiplex tags. Missing evidence remains missing and can never
become a negative observation.

## Standards and adapter pins

The following pins are normative only for their named adapters:

| Adapter | Pin | M01-02 mapping and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| W3C provenance export | [PROV-DM](https://www.w3.org/TR/2013/REC-prov-dm-20130430/), [PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/2013/REC-prov-o-20130430/), and [PROV-CONSTRAINTS](https://www.w3.org/TR/2013/REC-prov-constraints-20130430/), Recommendations, 30 April 2013 | Nodes export as entities, transformations/checks as activities, and governed actors/systems as agents. PROV's open-world semantics do not enforce M01-02 identity closure. |
| Proteomics relationship import/export | [SDRF-Proteomics v1.0.1](https://github.com/bigbio/proteomics-sample-metadata/releases/tag/v1.0.1), 29 August 2024 | `source name`, `assay name`, and data-file fields are scoped aliases/projections, never canonical patient lineage. The unreleased v1.1 development text is not normative. The adapter is independently implemented; no GPL validator code or specification text is copied. |
| Clinical-genomics export | [GA4GH Phenopacket schema 2.0.0](https://github.com/ga4gh/phenopacket-schema/releases/tag/2.0.0), 21 October 2021 | `Biosample.id`, `individual_id`, and `derived_from_id` form a privacy-filtered projection. Its single-parent biosample relation cannot replace the internal graph or pool semantics. |
| Variant evidence | [GA4GH VRS 2.0.1](https://github.com/ga4gh/vrs/releases/tag/2.0.1), 20 March 2025 | Normalized allele identifiers may identify loci in a fingerprint manifest; they never identify a person. |
| Pathology specimen import/export | [DICOM PS3 2026c](https://dicom.nema.org/medical/dicom/current/), 18 June 2026 publication | Issuer plus Specimen ID is an external scoped alias; Specimen UID, container ID, and preparation chronology remain distinct. DICOM/SNOMED licensing is not extended by this module. |
| Study/assay import/export | [ISA Model and Serialization Specifications 1.0](https://isa-specs.readthedocs.io/), 28 October 2016 | ISA Source/Sample/Extract/Assay/Data chains are projections retaining source namespace, version, and artifact digest. ISA is CC BY-SA 4.0; the core does not copy its schemas or prose. |

Every adapter records its exact release or commit, source artifact digest, source namespace,
and limitations. A standards label alone is insufficient provenance. Ontology labels are display
text; stable term identifiers and a pinned ontology artifact digest carry vocabulary identity.

## Evidence gate

Gate G0 requires:

- a closed synthetic fixture manifest with positive, negative, hostile, capacity-boundary, and
  privacy-canary cases;
- complete coverage of the seven node kinds and the exact contract transition table;
- poisoned-bridge, cross-patient, cycles, diamonds, pooling, demultiplexing, collision,
  duplicate-evidence, missingness, and forbidden-output checks;
- Unicode confusable, bidirectional-control, leading/trailing whitespace, empty-string, and
  normalization-boundary cases without permissive cleanup;
- metamorphic input-order, hash-seed, duplicate-evidence, and canonical-digest invariance;
- locked zero, one, maximum, and first-excess resource cases;
- blinded deterministic swap/collision/mixture hypotheses that always abstain or quarantine when
  evidence is insufficient;
- an executable eval report and linear-scaling microbenchmarks that record environment and sample
  counts; and
- external reviewer approval. Test code and implementation code cannot self-issue release
  approval.

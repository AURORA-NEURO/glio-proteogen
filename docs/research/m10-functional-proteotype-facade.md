# M10 fitted functional-proteotype evidence facade

`/v2/research/modules/m10/functional-proteotype` is an additive research-only
compatibility surface over `migliozzi-gbm-functional-proteotype/1.0.0`. It does
not modify or redirect any governed M10 route, schema, digest, or provisional
implementation.

The facade passes the delegated request, result, profile, uncertainty,
permutation family, ablations, and replay receipt through unchanged. Analysis
therefore runs the same source-locked joint robust fit over the exact Migliozzi
Table 2d GPM, MTC, NEU, and PPR bulk-protein signatures; no parallel formula or
facade-specific score exists. The HTTP path also shares the delegated adapter's
byte limits, concurrency semaphore, deadline, cancellation, strict JSON
ingress, and result-size enforcement.

The facade profile content-binds eight ordered M10 responsibility boundaries.
Only M10-03 and M10-07 receive
`research_numerical_stand_in_substitution_only`: supported fitted coordinates
may replace synthetic, hash-derived, fixed, or caller-declared numerical
stand-ins as research evidence. This is not a governed replacement. M10-01,
M10-02, M10-04, M10-05, M10-06, and M10-08 remain evidence-source-only or
outside the delegated model's responsibility.

The literal claim ceiling forbids sample pathway activation, posterior subtype,
mechanism, causal perturbation, prognosis, treatment guidance, and governed M10
replacement. Source Table 2e remains cohort context and never becomes a sample
pathway or categorical call.

The four stateless operations are:

- `GET /profile`
- `GET /demo`
- `POST /analyze`
- `POST /verify`

Profile, request, and result receipts are returned in `X-GLIO-*` headers and
responses are `Cache-Control: no-store`. The operation catalog marks the demo as
the exact validated synthetic request owned by the delegated model.

The underlying source, estimator, evaluation, and limitations are documented in
[`gbm-functional-proteotype.md`](gbm-functional-proteotype.md) and
[`gbm-functional-proteotype-source.md`](gbm-functional-proteotype-source.md).

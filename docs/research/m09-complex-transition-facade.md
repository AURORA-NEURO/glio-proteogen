# M09 complex-transition concordance evidence facade

`m09-complex-transition-concordance-evidence/1.0.0` is an additive,
research-only compatibility surface over
`kncc-reactome-complex-transition/1.0.0`. It adds no numerical path and does
not modify or redirect any governed M09 schema, digest, route, result, or
provisional implementation.

The delegated lane fits 28 separate, missing-aware robust rank-one transition
factors over exact Reactome V97 participant sets using 104 strict PDC000514
primary/recurrent GBM pairs. The facade passes its request, result, profile,
uncertainty, ablations, provenance, and replay receipt through unchanged. The
delegated request, profile, and result digests remain authoritative. A separate
facade-profile digest binds the eight ordered M09 responsibility mappings and
literal claim exclusions.

## HTTP surface

The central application mounts:

- `GET /v2/research/modules/m09/complex-transition-concordance/profile`
- `GET /v2/research/modules/m09/complex-transition-concordance/demo`
- `POST /v2/research/modules/m09/complex-transition-concordance/analyze`
- `POST /v2/research/modules/m09/complex-transition-concordance/verify`

All responses are non-cacheable. Profile, demo, analysis, and replay responses
carry `X-GLIO-Facade-Profile-Digest` and the authoritative delegated
`X-GLIO-Profile-Digest`. Analysis and replay retain the delegated request and
result digest headers. The facade shares the delegated strict JSON ingress,
2/4/8 MiB request/result/replay bounds, concurrency pool, cancellation,
deadline, safe errors, and exact replay verifier.

The v2 operation catalog derives all four operations from mounted FastAPI
routes and validates the exact delegated synthetic demo. The v1 deployment
catalog remains unchanged.

## Responsibility mapping

The facade profile maps every M09 responsibility exactly once and in order.
M09-02, M09-03, and M09-04 may use the fitted coordinate only to replace a
synthetic, fixed, caller-declared, hash-derived, or digest-derived
**participant-set transition-concordance** stand-in in research workflows.
They do not receive a complex-state, assembly, stoichiometry, essentiality, or
activity estimate. M09-05 is out of scope. M09-01, M09-06, M09-07, and M09-08
may cite the delegated receipt only as evidence and remain unsuperseded.

Reactome membership is annotation, not proof of physical interaction or a
mechanistic constraint. Internal held-member reconstruction, intervals,
support gates, and ablations are evidence diagnostics rather than external
calibration, clinical prediction, or intervention effects.

## Claim ceiling

The only positive numerical semantics are source-cohort Reactome participant-
set transition concordance. The facade cannot infer or claim:

- physical complex assembly, occupancy, or binding;
- stoichiometry or essential subunits;
- complex or biochemical activity;
- causal mechanism or intervention response;
- prognosis, survival, recurrence prediction, or treatment guidance;
- external validation, clinical class, or governed M09 replacement.

See [the fitted longitudinal complex-transition lane](longitudinal-gbm-complex-transition.md)
for source admission, fitting, validation, uncertainty, and limitation details.

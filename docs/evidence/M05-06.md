## Risk controls

- Strict duplicate-aware JSON decoding, exact media type, unknown-field
  rejection, no scalar coercion, and bounded canonical request/result bytes.
- Full M05-05 replay and disposition gate precede aggregate mapping; forged,
  stale, mutated, or unsupported upstream envelopes are rejected or safely
  abstained.
- Seven authorization controls are checked fail-closed; denied controls do
  not traverse upstream evidence or the observation ledger.
- Quarantine takes precedence over abstention; missing or unsupported input
  never becomes a negative result.
- The plugin validates once and issues a sealed, digest-bound token. Service,
  API, CLI, and library outputs derive from the same validated request.
- Output authority is closed to aggregate harmonization. Identity, consent,
  PTM/modification localization, kinase activity, all-omics fusion, treatment,
  and upstream mutation flags remain false.

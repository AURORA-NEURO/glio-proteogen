## Quality gates

The final record includes focused tests, Ruff check/format, strict MyPy on targeted files,
compileall, scoped branch-enabled coverage at or above 95%, evaluator 10/10, ten-iteration
benchmark within budget, isolated wheel import, release verifier, and generated-artifact cleanup.
These gates do not authenticate caller-declared evidence or freeze the provisional ABI.

## Replay-integrity hardening

Replay first validates the result's request identity and self-consistency, then
regenerates the complete operational result from the bound request and compares
canonical bytes. This covers the report, findings, provenance, evidence,
support decision, uncertainty, limitations, and terminal status. A caller that
changes a report or evidence record and recomputes `result_digest` is rejected;
the strict result contract also rejects an evaluated report whose declarations
do not match the exact request, and the evaluator/adversarial suite exercise
both direct parsing and replay for this semantic self-rehash case.

## Explicit exclusions

M23-07 does not emit variant-peptide estimates, infer identity or consent, make treatment decisions,
compute kinase activity, fuse generic all-omics inputs, authenticate issuer authority, or claim
future human-factors calibration. Unsupported and NOT_EVALUABLE inputs never become negative
evidence.

The focused matrix has 29 passing tests, including direct evaluator and
benchmark entrypoint regression coverage. The fresh ten-iteration benchmark
measured 3,397,740 ns mean, 3,239,300 ns median, and 3,604,500 ns p95
against 500,000,000/750,000,000 ns budgets.

# M03-02 evidence inventory

This proportionate G0 package verifies deterministic identity and lineage reconciliation for one
synthetic protein-inference workflow. It checks exact upstream closure, physical and artifact graph
relations, discrepancy propagation, and categorical CN-concordance semantics. It does not establish
a real identity, observe a peptide or protein, resolve an accession, estimate copy number, infer
activity, or validate a biological or clinical conclusion.

## Locked risk controls

- Raw authorization is evaluated before any lineage, artifact, or concordance field is traversed.
  Withheld consent or unresolved upstream authorization cannot be masked by a hostile nested object.
- The request closes over exact self-consistent M01-02 and M03-01 results. The eval executes M01-02,
  binds its exact resolution digest into M03-01, executes M03-01, then binds both into M03-02.
  Context bindings, embedded result digests, protocol identity, and search-space identity must agree
  without version fallback. A valid unresolved identity result abstains; a valid nonconformant
  protocol result quarantines. Stale or mismatched digest structure is rejected.
- The canonical profile covers all seven physical entity kinds while valid requests may retain any
  governed M01-02 path shape. Opaque upstream identifiers and
  subject components are reused exactly; the module cannot issue a new same-as link, merge a
  component, guess a missing identity, or rewrite the physical lineage.
- Physical lineage and the workflow-artifact DAG remain separate. The canonical artifact DAG is
  acyclic, endpoint-closed, role-compatible, and deterministically ordered. It contains exactly the
  four locked roles: peptide evidence -> protein group -> ambiguity, with the complex-activity input
  bundle receiving exactly one protein-group parent and one ambiguity parent.
- A swap, collision, duplicate-content assignment, or cross-patient link is retained as evidence.
  No finding authorizes relabelling, deduplication, deletion, or selection of a trusted copy.
- Cross-patient contamination propagates through every reachable artifact descendant in the
  submitted DAG while the upstream single-subject peptide roots remain unflagged. The closed DAG
  permits no disconnected branch. Propagation is a quarantine relation, not a newly inferred
  patient assignment.
- Dangling edges, cycles, self-dependencies, invalid roles, duplicate identifiers, ambiguous
  producers, and stale graph digests fail closed rather than being repaired.
- Upstream identity, M03-01 protocol, and search-space drift are distinct. A matching display label
  cannot make a stale digest current.
- CN-to-protein concordance is categorical control evidence. Concordant evidence corroborates only;
  discordant evidence preserves disagreement; missing, indeterminate, and unsupported evidence
  abstains. None of these states merges entities or establishes protein presence/absence.
- Strict reconstruction rejects coercion, unknown fields/terms, structural omission, duplicates,
  first-excess collections, and stale derived digests. Semantic reordering preserves the complete
  result, not merely its status.
- The recursive output boundary excludes raw/direct identifiers, raw tokens, observed peptides,
  sequences, protein accessions, raw copy-number or abundance values, activity scores, subtype,
  proteotype, kinase state, omics fusion, treatment advice, and clinical claims.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The locked corpus contains exactly eight scenario groups. Every named case must execute. The
canonical request must reconcile without discrepancy and must produce a completely equal result
after reordering every semantically unordered input collection. Each mutation changes a declared
boundary and has an explicit expected disposition, finding, abstention, authorization rejection, or
strict validation rejection.

The collision/duplicate scenarios assert retention of every distinct declaration. The
cross-patient scenarios assert downstream propagation and an unaffected-branch control. The CN
scenario covers all five closed states and forbids any component merge. The structural scenario
includes both the maximum supported shape and the first excess. Derived-digest checks cover stale
embedded upstream bindings, protocol/search-space drift, stale graph identity, and mutations whose
outer digest is not allowed to mask inconsistent inner content.

All fixtures are synthetic and non-clinical. They use opaque identifiers, categorical states, and
digest references only. Passing these criteria supports reproducible software behavior within the
locked profile; it does not authenticate caller-declared authorities, attest laboratory execution,
or establish scientific performance.

Recovery means correcting the governed source, creating a new immutable M01-02/M03-01 result when
needed, and issuing a new reconciliation request while retaining the discrepant evidence. Swaps,
collisions, duplicates, cross-patient propagation, discordance, override, claim promotion, novel
use, release exception, and unresolved biological conflict require external human review.

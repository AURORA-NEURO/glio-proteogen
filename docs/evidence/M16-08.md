## Safety and authority controls

- Seven caller-declared controls are inspected before typed request traversal; denial fails closed.
- M16-07 is referenced by exact media/version and is never relabeled, mutated, or replaced by a
  compact substitute. Unsupported and not-evaluable states never become negative findings.
- A critical discrepancy or policy violation is visible and review-required; routine drift is
  suspended and review-required; healthy continuation is only emitted for supported signals.
- Evidence, provenance, limitations, support, and all seven uncertainty dimensions remain explicit.
- KINOPHOS kinase ownership, generic all-omics fusion, treatment recommendation, and identity or
  consent inference are outside this module's output ceiling.

The package is provisional and cannot self-approve. A governed release must review the exact source
commit, fixture/evaluation/benchmark/coverage/package digests, authority evidence, and any critical
or unresolved finding before promotion.

## Locked risk controls

- Seven upstream control states are checked before source fields are traversed.
- Source artifact payloads remain opaque and caller-declared.
- Unsupported, incomplete, failed or non-evaluable conditions never emit a
  dossier or negative biological finding.
- Result identity binds the request digest; result digest binds canonical result
  content; a replay verifier rejects tampering.
- The parent output is named `variant_peptide` but `emits_parent` is always
  false, so this module cannot silently replace its upstream owner.
- Human review is required for both ready and abstained provisional results.

The gates are structural and synthetic. They do not prove upstream data
authenticity, calibration, transportability, biological mechanism, treatment
benefit, KINOPHOS behavior, or clinical validity.

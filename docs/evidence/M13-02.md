## Locked safety controls

The seven caller-declared controls are checked before request materialization.
Artifact references are content-addressed but opaque; the runtime does not
follow paths, URLs, raw payloads or embedded scientific content. A failed
preflight raises a sanitized authorization failure. A missing, unresolved or
conflicted required context dimension returns an abstained result with no
profile and no applicable mechanism set. No unsupported state is recoded as a
negative finding.

The canonical request digest binds the complete typed request, including policy,
observations, source references and mechanism candidates. The result digest
binds the complete output envelope. Revalidation rejects request-digest,
result-digest, status, profile, mechanism and evidence tampering.

## Acceptance and review boundary

The fixture evaluator is a deterministic engineering gate, not evidence of
biological validity, calibration, transportability or clinical utility. The
nominal 90% uncertainty target is recorded as a provisional acceptance criterion;
coverage must be monitored by context stratum before ABI promotion. Release
evidence requires owner review and an externally governed identity/consent
decision; this module cannot self-approve or freeze its own endpoint.

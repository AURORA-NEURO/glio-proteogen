# M02-06 evidence inventory

This G1 package tests the C02-specific harmonization boundary with deterministic synthetic data.
It does not self-issue reviewer approval and makes no claim of scientific or clinical
qualification.

## Locked risk controls

- All seven caller controls are checked before prerequisite or observation traversal.
- The request contains exact M02-01 through M02-05 outputs; their digests and dispositions are
  carried forward as receipts rather than reduced to caller-authored booleans.
- M02-05 exclusions form a one-way firewall. Excluded observations are removed from every control
  estimate, remain `excluded`, have no harmonized numeric value, and have no stage adjustments.
- Only observed, eligible controls contribute to medians. Missing, censored, not-applicable,
  unsupported, and excluded values are never imputed or represented as zero.
- All eight technical factors appear exactly once in the pinned order. Each transformation binds
  its stage configuration, pre-stage values, shifts, and post-stage values by digest.
- Applied shifts are exact when within the bound. A shift reaching the cap is reported as capped
  and quarantines even if later arithmetic happens to reduce spread.
- Each technical diagnostic compares actual pre/post control-level spread. Insufficient controls
  remain non-evaluable; unreduced or out-of-tolerance effects fail.
- Protected direction and rank are evaluated before and after the complete pipeline. A zeroed,
  inverted, non-evaluable, or excessive-change control is not accepted.
- Full results are canonical under permutations that do not change request semantics.
- Output is metadata and transformed abundance only. It cannot contain spectra, peptide rows,
  inferred subtype/proteotype, kinase activity, generic fusion, treatment advice, or upstream
  mutation.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The conformant fixture must produce eight technical diagnostics with positive pre-stage spread,
strictly lower post-stage spread, post-stage spread within the declared tolerance, and `passed`
status. Its direction and rank controls must retain the same non-zero sign and remain within their
declared tolerance. The typed-state fixture must preserve one example each of missing, censored,
not-applicable, and unsupported without a synthesized numeric value; the censoring bound must be
unchanged. The exclusion fixture must prove an exact M02-05-excluded target contributes to no
shift and receives neither a value nor an adjustment.

The locked conformant matrix contains 64 targets by two control features (128 observations). Each
target declares all eight factor levels. Synthetic values are the sum of a feature baseline, a
protected `0.75` case-group effect, and factor-specific comparison-level offsets. The stage
control-median estimates therefore have a strict positive pre-stage spread and an exact zero
post-stage spread for each factor. The direction score remains exactly `0.75`; the within-group
feature-rank score remains exactly `2.0`. These are fixture arithmetic assertions, not estimates
of biological validity. Exact upstream prerequisites are generated by executing the public
M02-01, M02-02, M02-03, M02-04, and M02-05 entry points; M02-05 evaluates all 64 target IDs (65 in
the exclusion scenario) rather than supplying caller-authored mask booleans.

Negative fixtures must abstain, quarantine, or boundary-reject precisely as declared. Unsupported
input, inadequate controls, and an unacceptable prerequisite abstain because a required estimate
cannot be evaluated. Capped shifts and direction/rank violations quarantine because an evaluable
transformation failed a hard acceptance control. Withheld consent is boundary-rejected before
hostile observations are iterated or sized. Canonical replay compares the complete typed result,
not a selected subset of fields.

These criteria describe deterministic regression on synthetic values only. They do not estimate
real-world effect removal, sensitivity, specificity, posterior probability, uncertainty coverage,
biological preservation, assay validity, transportability, or clinical performance. No learned
encoder is trained, fitted, or calibrated.

Recovery means correcting the authoritative prerequisite, observation, profile, control set,
invariant, policy, or upstream control and rerunning. Prior evidence is not rewritten. Critical
discrepancy, novel/OOD state, support override, claim promotion, release exception, and unresolved
biological conflict require governed human review outside M02-06.

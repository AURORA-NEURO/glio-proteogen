## Risk controls

- Preflight checks consent, identity/lineage, approved configuration, provenance, quality,
  support, and intended-use decisions before feature/invariant traversal.
- Unsupported, missing, unknown, or non-evaluable values abstain; they are never converted into
  a negative finding.
- Hard invariant violation and soft conflict are distinct statuses; soft conflict remains visible
  and limited rather than silently discarded.
- The module cannot emit the parent claim, kinase activity, generic all-omics fusion, treatment
  recommendation, or identity/consent inference.
- Canonical bytes and SHA-256 result digests are recomputed for replay; tamper and non-canonical
  payloads fail closed.

## Review and rollback

Owner confirmation, external scientific review, and release approval remain required. Corrections
must be append-only: publish a new request/result pair with a supersession digest; never overwrite,
re-sign, relabel, or silently promote an earlier result.

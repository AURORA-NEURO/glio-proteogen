## Runtime and safety assertions

- Seven caller controls are checked before graph traversal: approved configuration, identity
  lineage, provenance, consent, quality, support, and intended use. Rejected controls fail closed.
- Operation authorization, disabled operations, unresolved async jobs, and unresolved
  compatibility rules produce typed findings and abstention; they never become negative evidence.
- Results carry seven explicit `not_estimable` uncertainty dimensions, compact evidence,
  provenance/control decisions, support status, human-review requirement, and a provisional ABI flag.
- Canonical request/result digests and the derived result identifier are replay-checked. Tampering,
  unknown fields, duplicate JSON keys, foreign plugin tokens, and output overwrite attempts reject.
- API, SDK, CLI, service, engine, and plugin use the same strict parse-once contract path. API/CLI
  errors are sanitized and do not expose stack traces or private payloads.

## Scope and release limitations

The package publishes access metadata only. It does not authenticate caller authority, execute an
external model, inspect linked scientific content, infer identity or consent, or assert complex
activity biology. The ABI remains provisional until the dossier owner confirms the endpoint and
catalogue. CI status must be interpreted separately from these local gates; repository Actions may
be unavailable when account billing/spending-limit provisioning prevents jobs from starting.

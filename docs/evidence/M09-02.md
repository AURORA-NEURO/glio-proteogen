The implementation is a provisional G1/S2 engineering gate. Passing tests and benchmark are
software evidence only; they do not authenticate issuers, establish feature truth, validate a
probabilistic model, demonstrate 90% coverage, or authorize owner-sensitive biological or clinical
claims.

# M18-02 release evidence inventory

This package verifies deterministic cross-source alignment over synthetic caller-declared
metadata. It preserves seven alignment dimensions, source identity, conflicts, discrepancy
severity/resolution, support status, uncertainty, provenance, limitations, canonical replay, and
tamper detection. It does not prove biological truth, issuer authority, calibration,
transportability, identity, consent validity, clinical utility, or production ABI approval.

## Safety controls

- Seven caller-declared controls are checked before alignment observations are traversed; malformed
  or denied controls fail closed.
- The exact provisional M18-01 input media type is required and is never relabeled or replaced.
- Sample/time/territory/analyte/modality/reference/biological-context disagreement remains typed
  and reviewable; it is never erased or converted into a negative finding.
- Conflicted, incomplete, unsupported, or unresolved inputs produce safe abstention without an
  aligned bundle; uncertainty explicitly becomes not-estimable.
- The parent target is recorded but never emitted; kinase, generic all-omics, treatment, identity,
  and consent inference remain outside the output ceiling.

The ABI is provisional and cannot self-approve. A governed release must review the exact source,
fixture, evaluator, benchmark, coverage, package, authority, and critical-finding evidence.

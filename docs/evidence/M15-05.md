## Safety and scientific boundary

- Caller controls are checked before observations or configuration are traversed.
- Inputs are typed, bounded, strictly ordered by sequence and aware timestamp,
  and bound to the provisional M15-04 result media type.
- Source, feature, model, and evidence references remain opaque; no source bytes
  are read, hashed, or interpreted by this module.
- Trajectory labels are caller-declared artifact identifiers, not biological
  state estimates. Every emitted change point is `not_evaluable`.
- Identity, consent, treatment response, kinase activity, generic all-omics
  fusion, treatment recommendation, and unsupported-to-negative conversion are
  outside the output ceiling.

proof. Automated evidence is accepted only when the listed command passes for the reviewed
revision. All fixture identities and molecular observations are synthetic and non-clinical.

## Locked risk controls

- Strict decoding and frozen contracts reject duplicate keys, coercion, null-for-missing,
  non-finite numbers, unknown properties, invalid discriminator values, and the first byte/item
  above every cap.
- Identifiers are opaque and scoped. Whitespace, Unicode normalization, homoglyphs, and
  bidirectional controls are never silently cleaned into equality. Invalid boundary forms fail
  closed; distinct accepted code-point sequences remain distinct unless the contract explicitly
  declares a canonical form.
- Only authorized explicit `same_as` assertions may union. Tokens, content digests, concordance,
  proximity, labels, and graph neighbourhoods cannot merge identities.
- A hostile bridge poisons the entire connected identity component atomically. Cross-kind,
  different-from, token-collision, and multiple-patient-anchor contradictions cannot be hidden
  by union order or a selected representative.
- Iterative topology checks cover cycles, converging diamonds, repeated edges, depth boundaries,
  ordinary cross-patient edges, and pool/demultiplex policy. Material targets cannot acquire
  multiple undeclared producers; multi-input derivation is explicit pooling, while computed
  derived objects may retain a converging provenance diamond.
- Concordance deduplication binds the root observation, complete evidence-digest set, unordered
  endpoints, method/version, assay lineage, panel, reference, target, and evidence-policy version,
  not object position or artifact labels. Repetition cannot increase support or erase
  counter-evidence. Missing, ambiguous, and excluded observations remain separate and can only
  increase uncertainty or force abstention.
- Privacy canaries prove that raw genotype/allele/peptide values, direct patient identifiers,
  sex, ancestry, kinship, diagnosis, treatment, and module-external scientific findings are absent
  from public outputs, persisted evidence, diagnostics, and benchmark labels.
- Stable ordering and canonical digests are invariant to semantically unordered request
  collections and Python hash seed. The public graph never exposes disjoint-set representatives.
- Every finding exposes its complete causal-reference count, bounded representatives, and complete
  causal-basis digest. Provenance cross-binds the seven authorizing controls (granted consent and
  six accepted upstream decisions), their seven role-labeled evidence references, the
  request/policy/core/graph digests, and the complete request evidence-manifest digest.
- Size, locus, depth, component, evidence, issue, and body ceilings prevent recursive, quadratic,
  all-pairs, and unbounded-diagnostic behavior. Overflow is one stable quarantine issue rather
  than silently truncated ordinary output.
- The executable qualification gate drives every contract-accepted locked scenario through strict
  typed validation, the public pure solver, immutable service commitment, order-permuted exact
  replay, retrieval, standard JSON Schema validation, and exhaustive event-chain verification.
  Denied states for all seven controls are instrumented to prove rejection precedes analysis,
  canonical hashing, and persistence. A separate valid demultiplexing canary commits unique token,
  tag, entity-evidence, and channel-evidence markers and proves they are absent from both the
  public resolution and persisted event payload.

## Recovery and rollback protocol

1. Stop promotions/releases and retain the exact versioned request, artifact digests, request
   evidence-manifest digest, finding causal-basis digests and counts, policy digest, solver
   version, output digest, independently retained event count/head digest, and governed review
   record.
2. Re-run strict contract validation and canonical digest verification. Never repair identity
   data in place to make replay pass.
3. If only the derived SQLite projection, checkpoint, or immutability triggers are damaged,
   invoke the event store's exact-anchor recovery with the independently retained count and head.
   Recovery authenticates every immutable event first, rebuilds only derived state atomically,
   and refuses a wrong anchor, changed event, branch, truncation, or invalid payload.
4. Replay the prior reviewed solver against the retained immutable request. Exact replay must
   reproduce the prior output digest; disagreement is quarantined as evidence.
5. Roll application code back to that reviewed version. Do not rewrite upstream labels, delete
   counter-evidence, split a poisoned component, or select a new patient representative.
6. Preserve the failed output and comparison report. Reprocessing under a later contract creates
   a new versioned resolution linked by provenance; it does not replace history.

## Reviewer checkpoint

| Field | Required release value |
| --- | --- |
| Reviewed revision | Commit SHA |
| Reviewer | Named person or governed review identity |
| Evidence result | Full CI, eval, and benchmark artifact identifiers |
| Fixture attestation | Synthetic-only corpus; privacy canaries pass |
| Standards decision | Confirms pinned sources are adapters, not internal identity authority |
| Boundary decision | Confirms no upstream relabel, disagreement erasure, inferred identity/consent, kinase/fusion/treatment output, or missing-to-negative conversion |
| Decision | Approve or reject, with date and rationale |

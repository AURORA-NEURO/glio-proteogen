## Risk-control closure

- Seven control references are inspected before request traversal; hostile
  objects fail closed.
- A complete M07-06 result must be supported, decomposed, sensitivity-
  evaluated, and within the provisional 85–95% envelope.
- Every required calibration dimension must have samples, observed coverage,
  and an error no greater than 0.10. Target coverage remains nominal 90%.
- Candidate support, OOD, and calibration-error thresholds are checked before
  selection. Unknown strata, duplicate labels, missing numeric labels, and
  candidate shape violations cannot become predictions.
- No candidate passing the gate produces an explicit abstention with no
  estimates or prediction sets and a safe support status.
- Canonical request/result digests are recomputed on validation. Semantic
  reorderings preserve the digest; altered derived content fails replay.
- Caller-declared evidence is retained as evidence only. No external artifact
  is opened and no scientific, clinical, kinase, treatment, or parent output
  claim is emitted.

## Review and rollback

Release evidence is candidate evidence, not approval. External review must
cover the provisional ABI, calibration catalogue, OOD/support thresholds,
subgroup coverage, any override, and any claim promotion. Corrections are
append-only supersessions with new request/result digests and evidence; prior
results are never overwritten or relabeled.

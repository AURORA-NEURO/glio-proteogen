## Preregistered evaluator

The fixture contains exactly nine unique case identifiers. The evaluator requires declared and
executed case counts to match and requires every check to pass. Unsupported and review-required
scenarios are safe abstentions and never become negative evidence. The M22-05 dependency is checked
as a media-type and retained-artifact boundary only; no unpublished upstream service import is used.

## Quality gates

The final record must include focused tests, Ruff check/format, strict MyPy on targeted files,
compileall, branch-enabled scoped coverage at or above 95%, evaluator 9/9, ten-iteration
benchmark within budget, isolated wheel import, release verifier, and clean generated-artifact
removal. These gates do not authenticate caller-declared evidence or freeze the provisional ABI.

## Explicit exclusions

M22-06 does not emit protein-RNA estimates, infer identity or consent, make treatment decisions,
compute kinase activity, fuse generic all-omics inputs, authenticate issuer authority, or claim
future calibration/coverage. The result limitations and seven not-estimable uncertainty dimensions
remain visible in both evaluated and abstained outputs.

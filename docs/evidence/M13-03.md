## Typed glioma evidence lane

The numerical constructor is now driven by explicit `MechanisticObservation`
records rather than artifact identifiers. Observed and left-censored signed
effects are fit on a fixed, reviewable glioma graph spanning RTK/PI3K/AKT/mTOR,
p53/cell-cycle, IDH/HIF1A, proliferation, and mesenchymal programs. Damped
Huber IRLS coordinate descent preserves signed edge consistency and robustly
downweights discordant measurements; missing and unsupported records are
excluded, never treated as negative evidence. Deterministic request-digest
seeded bootstrap perturbations provide replayable intervals and a topology
support summary. Legacy opaque requests abstain, and kinetics or spatial
claims remain explicitly not estimable without those assays. This is a
research-use-only mechanistic association model, not a clinical or causal
inference.

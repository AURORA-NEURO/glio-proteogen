## Locked risk controls

- Seven caller controls are authorized before request traversal. Denied consent,
  malformed context, or unsupported upstream media fails closed.
- The upstream binding is media-type and artifact based. M19-08 does not invent or
  authenticate an M19-07 service and does not replace a typed export with a receipt.
- Telemetry, support drift, workflow effects, and discrepancies retain their declared
  status. `FAIL`, `NOT_EVALUABLE`, and unresolved discrepancy are not silently treated
  as healthy or negative biological evidence.
- Critical failures use the locked rollback threshold. Suspension and rollback are
  explicit state/decision pairs; each state has exactly one permitted decision.
- Unsupported or insufficient support returns `ABSTAINED` without a health report and
  with an explicit abstention reason. The result remains replayable and reviewable.
- Every observation and discrepancy ID is unique across categories. Finding IDs and
  finding codes are unique, preventing duplicate evidence from changing precedence.
- Result digests bind canonical request and result content. Identifier forgery,
  recomputed-payload forgery, replay mismatch, and invalid digest are rejected.
- Seven uncertainty dimensions remain explicit and not estimable. Runtime latency is
  not evidence of monitoring accuracy or rollback effectiveness.
- API, CLI, service, plugin, and library boundaries share strict reconstruction and
  canonical result semantics. Duplicate keys and oversized JSON fail before execution.
- Rollback is a decision record only. Recovery requires external governance and a new
  immutable superseding result; no previous result or evidence is mutated or erased.

## Evaluation and benchmark record

The evaluator executes the locked healthy, degraded, rollback-required, suspended,
not-evaluable, denied-control, rejected-upstream, and replay-tamper scenarios. It also
checks critical telemetry/support/workflow paths, unresolved discrepancies, explicit
seven-dimensional uncertainty, and parent-output suppression. All 8 scenario oracles
and all 8 adversarial checks passed.

The benchmark constructs the request outside timing, performs one warm-up, and times
25 public `M1908TranslationMonitoringEngine.infer` calls. The observed sample is roughly
2.9 ms mean and 3.3 ms p95 on the local environment, below the provisional 500 ms and
750 ms budgets. It is a software-regression tripwire, not a performance claim about
production telemetry or deployment infrastructure.

## Review and recovery

The implementation cannot self-approve a release, authenticate a signer, or promote a
rollback. A governed reviewer must independently review the exact source commit,
fixture/evaluator digest, result/replay evidence, support status, and rollback policy.
For critical drift or unresolved discrepancy, preserve the exact immutable request and
result, suspend downstream use, obtain external review, and publish only a new
superseding request after the source policy or upstream export is corrected. Never
overwrite, relabel, deduplicate, or erase the prior evidence.

## Gate interpretation

This is a provisional G1/S2 engineering gate. Passing tests, evaluator, coverage, and benchmark are
software evidence only; they do not authenticate issuers, establish a 90% calibration claim, prove
transportability, or permit owner-sensitive biological or clinical claims.

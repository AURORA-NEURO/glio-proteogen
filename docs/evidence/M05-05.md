## Safety and replay

The evaluator includes clear, seeded-critical, contamination-detected,
missing, unsupported, ledger-binding-only, quarantined-upstream, and
abstained-upstream scenarios. Hardening tests cover unknown-field rejection,
duplicate-key rejection, hostile mapping access, byte limits, nested digest
tampering, replay-context mismatch, token forgery, and safe abstention. No
scenario permits identity, consent, treatment, kinase, protein, proteoform,
isoform, glioma-specific, or all-omics inference.

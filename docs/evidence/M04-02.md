## Locked risk controls

- Seven caller controls are inspected before any governed result, policy, claim, derivation, or
  nested mapping. Every individual denial and arbitrary mapping is rejected with zero traversal.
  Built-in dict/subclass materialization bypasses overridden accessors; ordinary exceptions fail
  closed and BaseException is not swallowed.
- The request embeds exact full public M01-02 and M04-01 results and strictly replays both. Context
  identity, M01-02 resolution, M04-01 identity subject, intended-use evidence, configuration,
  policy, producer reference/coordinate bindings, and chronology close exactly. Compact receipts
  and handwritten substitutes are not accepted.
- The physical graph contains every seven entity kinds in the locked canonical request. Every
  claim anchors to a reachable derived object and exact upstream subject components. Physical
  lineage and the local artifact DAG remain separate and immutable.
- The artifact graph has five closed roles and one exact approved derivation. Its target is the
  sole bundle; its unique sources are all non-bundle claims and cover all four source roles.
  Missing roles, multiple targets, self/dangling endpoints, disconnected claims, duplicate sources,
  and unapproved methods reject without repair.
- Swaps compare declared and resolved upstream subject components. Cross-patient contamination is
  the multi-component source union, propagates only to the target bundle, and never creates a
  patient assignment. Findings do not relabel claims or rewrite edges.
- Binding-scope, artifact-identity, and lineage collisions remain distinct. Duplicate content is
  retained across both same and different scopes and never deduplicated or selected as authoritative.
  Quarantine findings take precedence over abstention findings.
- Producer identity, protocol, reference-bundle, and coordinate-policy drift remain separate exact
  findings. A valid unresolved M01-02 result abstains; a valid quarantined M04-01 result quarantines;
  malformed or re-signed upstream objects reject.
- Observed evidence can reconcile. Missing, indeterminate, unsupported, and redacted evidence
  abstains while preserving every component and declaration. No state creates protein presence or
  absence, CN regression, discordance, identity, or consent inference.
- Strict ingress rejects duplicate JSON keys, coercion, unknown fields/terms, role/MIME mismatch,
  omissions, contradictions, duplicates, first excess, canonical requests above 4 MiB, and stale
  derived values. The maximum 256-claim shape is structurally accepted, executes completely,
  and quarantines under the exact repeated-scope collision rules.
- Canonicalization applies only to semantic collections. Reordering yields complete request and
  result equality and stable digests; it cannot hide a duplicate.
- Result validation rederives the graph, findings, disposition, receipt, support, uncertainty,
  provenance, evidence, limitations, review, completion time, and result digest. The receipt's
  canonical unique finding-code set independently determines its disposition. Finding actions are
  code-locked: duplicate retention records; the three non-evaluable/unresolved codes abstain; all
  other codes quarantine. Re-signed graph, finding, provenance, evidence, receipt, or result
  regions reject.
- Recursive privacy tests require opaque namespaced identifiers and exact role media types and
  prove that a prohibited canary is absent from results. Recursive authority tests require every
  identity/protein/proteoform/CN/discordance/kinase/fusion/treatment/mutation flag to remain false.
- Recovery is append-only. A corrected request explicitly supersedes the immutable prior result
  without mutation, deletion, relabelling, deduplication, re-signing, or silent promotion.

## Contract and interface plan

The public registry exports exactly eight JSON Schema 2020-12 names: request, output, policy,
artifact-claim, derivation, graph, finding, and receipt. Unknown schema names fail closed.

The Python boundary is reconcile_proteoform_identity_lineage. HTTP exposes
POST /v1/modules/M04-02/identity-lineage-reconciliation and
GET /v1/contracts/M04-02/{name}/schema. CLI exposes
proteoform-lineage reconcile REQUEST and proteoform-lineage export-schema NAME. Successes,
quarantine, abstention, strict reconstruction failure, authorization failure, canonical JSON, and
digests must be equivalent across applicable interfaces. No interface accepts a looser request,
bypasses preflight, or adds binary/filesystem ingestion.

## Benchmark criterion

The benchmark builds genuine public M01-02 and M04-01 outputs and the representative seven-kind,
five-claim, one-derivation M04-02 request before measurement. One untimed public call verifies
reconciliation, physical-node count, claim/derivation shape, findings, evidence, limitations,
parent context, false authority flags, and determinism. It then measures exactly 25 public
reconcile_proteoform_identity_lineage calls; upstream construction, request construction,
serialization, imports, CLI startup, HTTP transport, and reporting stay outside the timed boundary.
The nearest-rank p95 sample is used. Passing requires mean no more than 400,000,000 ns and p95 no
more than 600,000,000 ns.

The 256-claim maximum is exercised by the eval capacity cases, not this representative benchmark.
Latency provides no evidence of identity truth, protein/proteoform inference, model accuracy,
uncertainty coverage, calibration, robustness, distribution shift, biological validity,
transportability, human factors, or prospective use.

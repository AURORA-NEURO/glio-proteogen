# M03-01 evidence inventory

This proportionate G0 package verifies deterministic conformance of one synthetic
protein-inference protocol. It checks protocol declarations and relational closure only. It does
not execute peptide-to-protein inference, estimate target-decoy error, establish reference truth,
or validate a biological conclusion.

| Evidence | Artifact | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locked eight-group corpus | `tests/fixtures/m03_01/scenarios.json` | search-space closure, assignment/group ambiguity, variant discrimination, error-control compatibility, aliases/unresolved states, canonical strictness, authorization order, and closed handoff |
| Contract/module tests | M03-01 focused tests | strict schemas, closed vocabularies, derived digests, relational invariants, quarantine, and hard rejection |
| Executable eval | `evals/m03_01/run.py` | public-constructor replay of all eight groups, semantic reorder equality, typed quarantine, authorization preflight, and recursive output-boundary checks |
| Representative benchmark | `benchmarks/m03_01_protocol_metadata.py` | public evaluator over a representative complete protocol with exact section and seven-role handoff assertions |
| Traceability | `docs/traceability/GLIO-PROTEOGEN-M03-01.csv` | requirement-to-contract-to-executable-evidence mapping |
| Scientific and clinical qualification | Governed downstream processes | explicitly outside this module and evidence package |

## Locked risk controls

- Authorization precedes protocol-section traversal. A hostile unreadable section cannot mask
  withheld consent or unresolved identity.
- Control decisions, reference receipts, review identifiers, and digests are caller-declared.
  Their exact binding is tested, but the module does not authenticate issuers, prove reference
  truth, or confer approval authority.
- The profile and protocol version are exact. An unapproved applicability, assay/specimen
  version, controlled-vocabulary version, unit-system version, search-space build, error-control
  policy, assignment strategy, grouping rule, or eligibility bound returns a typed quarantined
  result; no nearest-profile or unversioned fallback is selected.
- Search-space composition and build identity bind exact digest-bearing reference receipts. The
  protocol must declare that alias collisions remain unresolved and that unversioned guesses are
  forbidden. Missing mandatory structure is rejected; M03-01 does not resolve observed aliases.
- Decoy construction and error-control strategy must be compatible. Passing compatibility does
  not claim that an FDR was estimated, calibrated, or controlled for real observations.
- Uniqueness-relative-to-search-space, shared-group-only, and razor non-promotion rules are
  distinct declarations. Shared or razor evidence never authorizes a unique member claim.
- Indistinguishable members are preserved. A representative is used for display and handoff,
  never promoted to exclusive observation or biological primacy.
- The protocol requires a pinned isoform/variant reference and eligible discriminating evidence
  before any later member claim. M03-01 validates that declaration but evaluates no observed
  peptide; unresolved or missing evidence is explicitly not negative.
- The output closes over exactly seven handoff roles and contains no activity score, inferred
  complex activity, protein subtype, proteotype, kinase activity, fused interpretation, treatment
  recommendation, or clinical claim.
- Semantic ordering is canonical. Strict reconstruction rejects unknown keys, coercion, stale
  derived digests, duplicate identifiers, and structural ownership violations.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The locked corpus contains exactly eight scenario groups. Each group isolates one ownership
boundary and names all constituent cases. A canonical protocol must be conformant and must remain
completely equal after reordering every semantically unordered collection. Reviewed-domain
mismatches must quarantine with section-specific findings. Required alias and unresolved-state
semantics are closed declarations, not observed records. Missing mandatory structure, invalid
enumerations, percent/fraction confusion, duplicates, excess cardinality, stale derived values,
or ownership claims must fail strict construction or evaluation and cannot be converted to
quarantine by silent repair. Consent denial must be observed before any hostile protocol mapping
is traversed.

The assignment scenarios validate the protocol's declared rules; they do not submit observed
peptide-spectrum matches for inference. Likewise, the variant scenario verifies that the
protocol requires a pinned reference and discriminating eligible evidence; it does not assert
that any real isoform or variant is present. Recovery means correcting the governed protocol,
profile, receipt, or authorization source and issuing a new validation request while retaining
the prior evidence. Critical discrepancy, override, claim promotion, novel/OOD use, or unresolved
biological conflict requires external human review.

so one declared source cannot alias another artifact with a different digest,
version, or media type.

The result provenance and evidence inventory also retain the exact digests of
configuration, observation, and discrepancy evidence nested under the request.
This prevents a replay-valid alignment result from omitting the declarations
that supplied its dimensional and conflict findings.

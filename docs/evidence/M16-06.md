## Locked safety and ownership controls

- All seven controls are checked before queue or evidence traversal.
- Assignments are explicitly blinded and addressed by opaque reviewer token;
  reviewer authority is caller-declared and not authenticated here.
- Every requested discrepancy must be covered by an assignment; every decision
  is retained in a locked, sequenced audit history.
- Critical unresolved, deferred, abstained, missing, or unsupported cases do
  not produce an adjudication record and remain review-required.
- Measurement, sampling, parameter, model-form, identification, support, and
  transport uncertainty remain explicitly not-estimable at this boundary.
- KINOPHOS kinase activity, generic all-omics fusion, treatment recommendation,
  identity/consent inference, upstream mutation, and disagreement erasure are
  outside the output ceiling.

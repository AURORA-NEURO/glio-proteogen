The runtime requires accepted configuration, resolved identity lineage,
accepted provenance, granted consent, accepted quality, accepted support, and
accepted intended-use controls before reading challenge data. Within-envelope
scenarios produce a typed robustness surface. Borderline, out-of-domain,
unsupported, corrupted, missing, or novel-state scenarios produce an
abstained result with a safe-failure report and human review requirement;
unsupported material is never converted into a negative biological claim.

Results carry OOD scores/bands, typed findings, seven uncertainty dimensions,
provenance, evidence, limitations, and canonical request/result digests. The
service, strict parse-once plugin, FastAPI routes, and Typer commands share
the same contract and sanitized replay behavior.

The ABI is explicitly `0.1.0-provisional`, S3, G3. M24-06 does not emit a
biomarker-panel conclusion, kinase state, generic all-omics fusion, treatment
recommendation, identity inference, consent inference, raw scientific-content
traversal, or an upstream mutation.

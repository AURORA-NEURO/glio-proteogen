# M02-07 evidence inventory

This proportionate G1 package tests the C02-specific joint support-envelope boundary with
deterministic synthetic data. It does not self-issue reviewer approval and makes no claim of
scientific or clinical qualification.

## Locked risk controls

- All seven caller controls are checked before prerequisite receipts or dimension evidence.
- Compact receipts are produced from genuine M02-04 and M02-06 public results. The M02-07 request
  neither embeds a full M02-06 result nor accepts caller-authored release booleans.
- A supported result requires both prerequisite receipts to be releasable and lineage-consistent.
- The eight declared dimensions are evaluated jointly against one envelope. A request cannot
  assemble a synthetic match by selecting different dimensions from different envelopes.
- Platform and reference are set-valued all-member constraints. Extra unsupported members abstain
  even when another declared member is supported.
- Missing, unknown, and outside-envelope evidence remain distinct reasons; none is converted into
  a biological negative.
- Every abstention retains its exact dimension and reviewed remediation in canonical order.
- Semantically unordered envelope declarations and member sets replay to the complete same typed
  result, not merely the same top-level decision.
- Output cannot contain raw spectra, peptide rows, harmonized abundance payloads, inferred subtype
  or proteotype, kinase activity, fusion interpretation, treatment advice, or upstream mutation.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The exact-joint case must be supported by one named envelope with no abstentions. The
outside-envelope matrix must produce one isolated typed reason for each of assay, specimen,
disease class, quality, completeness, platform, reference, and intended use. Missing and unknown
evidence must remain distinguishable. Each unreleasable prerequisite receipt must independently
prevent support. Platform/reference cases must prove all-member rather than any-member matching.
The cross-envelope case must abstain even though every individual value appears somewhere in the
profile. Reordered declarations, envelopes, allowed members, evidence, and receipts must yield
complete result equality. Withheld consent must reject before hostile evidence can be iterated or
sized.

These are exact fixture assertions, not estimates of sensitivity, specificity, false-support
rate, cohort coverage, transport performance, posterior probability, uncertainty coverage, assay
validity, or clinical utility. Recovery means correcting the authoritative receipt, declaration,
envelope, policy, or caller control and rerunning; previous evidence is not rewritten. Support
override, claim promotion, release exception, novel/OOD state, and unresolved biological conflict
require governed review outside M02-07.

## Safety disposition

Verification is deterministic and replay-bound: the `replay=False` compatibility
argument is rejected, and every accepted result is regenerated from its bound
request before semantic comparison. A caller cannot make a changed alignment
or review flag appear valid by recomputing only `result_digest`.

This is an engineering-provisional lane eligible for human review, not automatic clinical
promotion. Discrepancies, unsupported sources, unresolved identity/consent, and boundary markers
remain explicit and auditable. No upstream evidence is mutated, relabeled, erased, or converted
into a negative finding.

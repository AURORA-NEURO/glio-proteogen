## Safety controls

- Seven caller-declared controls are inspected before contribution traversal; denial fails closed.
- The exact M17-02 media/version reference is required and is never relabeled or replaced.
- Source identity, reliability, disagreement, and signed propagation are retained; no conflict is
  erased or converted into a negative conclusion.
- Low reliability and open disagreement remain integrated only with review-required support and
  human review. Not-evaluable contributions produce safe abstention without an integrated object.
- The parent target is recorded but never emitted; kinase, generic all-omics, treatment, identity,
  and consent inference remain outside the output ceiling.

The ABI is provisional and cannot self-approve. A governed release must review exact source,
fixture, evaluator, benchmark, coverage, package, authority, and critical-finding evidence.

## Safety controls

- Authorization is evaluated before request sections, artifacts, or configuration are traversed.
- Every view remains attributable to source artifacts and evidence; the service does not open
  external scientific content or authenticate caller declarations.
- Missing/unsupported support is abstention, never a negative finding. Unsupported results do
  not carry a workspace.
- The configuration requires all six view kinds and an automation-bias warning. Default order
  must begin with the task summary and cover every section exactly once.
- Results always require human review, preserve seven uncertainty dimensions, and explicitly
  retain limitations and prohibited-output boundaries.
- Result provenance binds each section's evidence and the locked configuration evidence,
  preserving the complete nested workspace lineage for replay and audit.
- Canonical digests and full replay detect result/request tampering; no overwrite or silent
  promotion path exists in the CLI.

## Non-claims

The evidence does not establish assay validity, biological truth, model accuracy, calibration,
clinical utility, transportability, reviewer performance, identity, consent, kinase activity,
all-omics fusion, treatment effect, or parent biomarker-panel release.

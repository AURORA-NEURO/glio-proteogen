## Locked risk controls

- All seven caller controls are checked before prerequisite receipts, declarations, context
  receipts, envelope membership, or evidence traversal. A denied context cannot be masked by
  malformed or hostile downstream accessors.
- Compact receipts are produced only by public helpers that strictly reparse genuine M04-04 and
  M04-06 results. The full strict results travel beside those receipts solely so M04-07 can
  rederive and prove the projections; it does not reinterpret or mutate them, independently
  authenticate an issuer, or prove external reference truth.
- Private replay capabilities are issued only after full prerequisite admission and bind exact
  request/prerequisite/upstream identities plus canonical and raw model snapshots, and seal the
  complete derived bundle with its own snapshot digest. Copies, replacements, stale-digest or
  stale-bundle mutation, or unissued reconstruction cannot use the sealed path; every public or
  unsealed request/result still performs full replay. Owned result validation compares every
  emitted field to the one complete deterministic bundle derived for that exact admitted request.
- Both prerequisites must be releasable and share the exact governed lineage. A quarantined,
  abstained, rejected, mismatched, absent, or stale receipt cannot become supported.
- Exactly eight dimensions are evaluated jointly against one reviewed envelope. Support cannot be
  assembled from dimensions admitted by different envelopes. Completeness is the conservative
  minimum of the qualified M04-04 raw-input-completeness projection and accepted M04-06
  evaluable-unit fraction; missing components stay indeterminate and observed below-envelope
  values route outside the domain.
- Platform and reference declarations use all-member matching. One admitted member cannot hide an
  unsupported companion.
- Missing and unknown caller evidence remain distinct indeterminate states. Neither becomes a
  biological negative, an empty observed set, or a supported value.
- Every outside or indeterminate dimension retains its exact fixed reason and reviewed remediation
  in canonical order; no arbitrary first-error collapse or nearest-term inference is permitted.
- Semantically unordered declarations, contexts, envelopes, approved versions, members, and
  evidence replay to the complete same typed result and stable field-owned digests.
- Request, profile, policy, envelope, declared-value, reason, remediation, evidence, reviewer, and
  route identifiers are content-derived opaque values with exact namespace and 64-lowercase-hex
  shape. Projected M04-06 platform IDs remain their exact upstream `level` identifiers.
- Strict ingress rejects duplicate keys, coercion, non-finite values, unknown fields or terms,
  missing required fields, contradictory state, stale derived values, every collection excess,
  and the first byte past the 4 MiB cap.
- Nested assessments, reasons, remediations, disposition, support, evidence, provenance, and
  digests are replayed for self-consistency. Re-signing an outer object cannot conceal a
  contradictory prerequisite or envelope decision.
- Library, engine, service, plugin, API, CLI, and all 14 installed schema exports must agree.
- Recovery is append-only: corrected governed inputs create a new immutable result with explicit
  supersession provenance. Prior evidence is not mutated or silently promoted.
- Output preserves `protein_rna_discordance` only as parent workflow context and emits no identity,
  consent, protein, proteoform, isoform, discordance, proteogenomic-state, proteotype,
  protein-level-subtype, kinase, abundance, calibrated-probability, all-omics-fusion, treatment,
  or clinical claim.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The locked corpus contains exactly eight groups and 19 unique case identifiers, allocated
`1/8/2/2/2/1/2/1`. The exact-joint case must be supported by one complete envelope with no
abstentions. The outside-envelope matrix covers assay, specimen, disease class, quality,
completeness, platform, reference, and intended use; completeness specifically proves that an
observed conservative upstream value below a stricter route-envelope minimum abstains rather
than being coerced to support. Missing and
unknown declarations must remain distinguishable. M04-04 and M04-06 unreleasable chains must each
prevent support: the M04-04 failure necessarily propagates into M04-06, while the standalone
M04-06 failure retains a releasable M04-04 result. Platform and reference cases must prove
all-member matching. The
cross-envelope case must abstain even though every value appears somewhere in the profile.
Canonical and semantically reordered requests must produce complete equal results. Withheld
consent must reject before hostile evidence can be iterated or sized.

These are exact fixture assertions, not estimates of sensitivity, specificity, false-support
rate, cohort coverage, transport performance, posterior probability, uncertainty coverage, assay
validity, biological truth, protein-RNA discordance, or clinical utility. Support override, claim
promotion, novel/OOD state, unresolved upstream conflict, and clinical interpretation require
governed human review outside M04-07.

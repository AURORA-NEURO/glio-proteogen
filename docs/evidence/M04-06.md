## Locked risk controls

- The seven authorization controls are evaluated before compact receipt or support-ledger
  traversal. A denied context cannot be masked by hostile mapping behavior, malformed nested
  content, an oversized graph, or a forged derived digest.
- Executable evidence reconstructs genuine M04-01, M04-02, M04-03, M04-04, and M04-05
  results through public operations. The exact M04-05 result, request, policy, configuration,
  artifact receipt and evidence ledger, all seven posterior projections per target, contamination
  flags, exclusion mask, disposition,
  support, and parent target are revalidated before the harness constructs support coordinates.
  Standalone M04-06 accepts caller-declared content-addressed compact evidence; it does not
  independently attest execution, authenticate a signer, or prove external reference truth.
- The support ledger closes over the M04-05 result, derived artifact receipt, exact projected-target
  binding, one observation per target, factor levels, protected invariants, evidence, and recorded
  time. Observation unit kind, aggregate target state, action, all seven posterior digests,
  contamination flags, and exclusion state must agree with the derived receipt.
- Request, policy, profile, ledger, target, anchor, group, level, invariant, stage, reviewer, and owned evidence
  identifiers are content-derived opaque values in the exact form `<namespace>.<64 lowercase hex>`.
  Output copies and cross-references reuse those exact values; caller labels cannot enter the
  reflected graph under an opaque-looking prefix. Owned evidence media types use strict lowercase
  `type/subtype` syntax.
- Every observation carries all eight technical factor levels. Every active profile carries one
  and only one ordered stage for each factor. Estimation and validation anchors are unique,
  disjoint, and known to the ledger.
- The artifact mask is a one-way firewall. Review and excluded targets neither train a shift nor
  receive a correction. Re-signing their compact projection cannot promote them into retained
  support without rebinding the exact ledger and satisfying recursive validation.
- Observed support uses bounded integer coordinates on the 1,000,000 scale. Each non-reference
  level shift is the exact signed lower median of paired estimation-anchor differences. No
  binary-floating-point arithmetic, caller-supplied shift, fitted model, probability, or abundance
  interpretation is trusted. A held-out post-correction residual alone may reach the exact
  2,000,000 difference ceiling when estimation and validation effects are opposite-signed.
- Stages execute sequentially. Every trace binds pre-stage coordinates, level shifts, applied
  adjustments, and post-stage coordinates. Exact shift-cap and coordinate-clamp boundaries are
  explicit review conditions rather than silent successful transformations.
- Missing, censored, not-applicable, unsupported, and observed-zero support remain distinct.
  Censoring upper bounds are preserved. A non-observed state cannot train a shift or acquire an
  invented numeric coordinate.
- Held-out validation anchors do not contribute to estimation. Technical acceptance requires
  residual reduction within tolerance. Insufficient pairs remain not evaluable and unreduced
  evaluable effects quarantine.
- Support direction, support rank, and composition fraction are replayed before and after all eight
  stages. A required invariant that cannot be evaluated causes abstention; an evaluable violation
  quarantines. These controls protect declared finite-corpus relationships, not biological truth.
- Quarantined and abstained M04-05 states propagate before support-ledger traversal.
  Complete receipts with excluded or review targets, receipt-ledger mismatches, unsupported profiles,
  capped shifts, clipping, technical failure, and invariant failure follow deterministic typed
  precedence and cannot emit accepted harmonization.
- Exactly one reviewed profile must match applicability plus assay-protocol, specimen-processing,
  controlled-vocabulary, and unit-system versions. Labels do not override governed content or
  version disagreement.
- Strict ingress rejects duplicate JSON keys, scalar coercion, non-finite values, unknown fields or
  terms, silent defaults, stale derived values, collection excess, and the first byte past the
  canonical cap. Installed exact maxima and first-excess boundaries execute.
- Semantic ordering cannot change the complete result. Typed, dictionary, and strict-JSON
  requests agree. Request, policy, profile, receipt, target-binding, ledger, stage, shift,
  adjustment, diagnostic, invariant, finding, and result digests remain stable.
- Every nested projection, binding, transformation, diagnostic, invariant, finding, disposition,
  and digest is replayed for self-consistency. Re-signing an outer object cannot mask a
  contradictory inner artifact action, support coordinate, shift, stage trace, diagnostic, or
  ledger binding.
- Library, engine, service, plugin, API, and CLI harmonization agree. API and CLI schema export
  return exact installed contracts with M04-06 URNs.
- Recursive ownership/privacy checks exclude direct identifiers, raw identity tokens, peptide
  sequences, accessions, protein or proteoform assertions, abundance, calibrated probability,
  protein-RNA discordance or kinase scores, subtype, proteotype, fused interpretation, treatment
  advice, and clinical decisions.
- Recovery is append-only: corrected support evidence, policy, profile, invariant, or upstream
  artifact result produces a new result with explicit supersession provenance; prior evidence is
  not mutated.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The locked corpus contains exactly eight groups and 56 unique case identifiers, allocated
7/9/7/7/7/7/6/6. Every named case must execute and no parameterized oracle may silently omit a
declared case. The canonical clean workload must execute all eight factor stages with exact integer
arithmetic, reduce held-out technical residuals within tolerance, preserve all three protected
invariant kinds, close every compact receipt and support-ledger binding, preserve the exact
`protein_rna_discordance` parent ceiling, and remain completely equal after semantic reordering and
across public surfaces. M04-05 review and excluded targets must receive exactly zero adjustments.

All examples are small, synthetic, and non-clinical. M04-06 graph identifiers are content-derived
and opaque, and facts are bounded fixed-point coordinates or categorical metadata states. No
example contains an observed peptide
sequence, real accession assignment, patient fact, protein abundance, calibrated probability,
activity score, or treatment information. Exact replay on this preregistered corpus proves only
deterministic software behavior. External authorities, laboratory execution, assay performance,
population effect removal, biological preservation, transportability, and clinical relevance
remain governed elsewhere.

Recovery requires correction of governed evidence or policy, execution of a new immutable request,
and explicit supersession provenance. Capped or clipped transformations, unsupported profiles,
unresolved artifact review, technical failure, invariant violation, novel use, result promotion,
or clinical interpretation require external human review.

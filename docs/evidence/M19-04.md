## Locked evaluator result

The evaluator output is deterministic at the semantic level and carries request/result digests.
Every declared scenario executes against an explicit oracle. Supported research emits only the
bounded `proteotype` object; clinical/release paths remain review-gated; blocked claims abstain
without an apparently negative object; all seven uncertainty dimensions stay explicit; and
tampered result payloads fail replay.

## Review and rollback

M19-04 cannot self-approve. External review must bind reviewer identity, decision time, exact
module/contract version, source commit, schema and fixture digests, evaluator/benchmark records,
and any exception or claim-promotion decision. Retain immutable evidence; correct only through a
new request and result naming the superseded digest; never overwrite, erase, relabel, or silently
promote prior output.

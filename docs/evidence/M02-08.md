supply chain.

## Locked risk controls

- All seven caller controls are checked before upstream outputs or caller artifact mappings are
  traversed, sized, hashed, or archived.
- The chain contains genuine strict M02-01 through M02-07 results. Each result remains internally
  digest-valid and immutable; packaging does not replace it with a caller-authored receipt shell.
- Required upstream stages remain distinct. Their disposition matrix proves that no one stage's
  acceptance masks another stage's quarantine or abstention.
- Identity, analysis lineage, upstream digest, stage order, and predecessor/successor receipts
  close across the whole chain; a valid result from a different chain is not composable.
- The parent receipt is strict and binds exactly `protein_subtype`, the packaged M02-02 subject,
  and the authorized intended-use evidence; wrong target, subject, or intended use is a hard input
  error before signature verification or archive construction.
- Eight caller members are exact: canonical relative path, media type, byte count, and SHA-256.
  The archive contains no missing, extra, aliased, duplicate, absolute, drive-qualified, or
  traversal member.
- The generated manifest is canonical before verification. The external signature statement
  binds that manifest digest; the generated verifier receipt records verification but is not an
  input to the signed digest, so no circular signature dependency exists.
- The injected deterministic verifier is a fixture seam. Tests assert its exact inputs, one call,
  typed success/failure, and replay behavior; they do not treat it as a cryptographic primitive or
  signer authority.
- Archive headers and order are deterministic. Reordered semantically unordered inputs produce
  complete result equality and byte-identical archive bytes.
- Strict reconstruction rejects coercion, unknown fields, invalid enumerations, stale digests,
  mismatched sizes, unsafe paths, malformed statements, unsupported algorithms, and excess shape.
- Output contains no caller artifact bytes, raw spectra, peptide rows, upstream scientific
  payloads, inferred subtype/proteotype, kinase activity, fusion interpretation, treatment advice,
  key material, signature secrets, or upstream mutations.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The canonical case must release one exact chain and produce ten archive members: eight caller
artifacts, one canonical manifest, and one verification receipt. Semantic reorder must preserve
the complete typed result and archive bytes. The seven-stage matrix must exercise one
non-releasable disposition per M02-01 through M02-07 and never emit a released package. A
cross-chain substitution must be rejected even when the substituted result is independently
valid. Integrity cases must isolate byte, digest, size, missing/extra member, duplicate/alias, and
path failures. Signature cases must prove exact manifest binding, unsupported/malformed/rejected
outcomes, one-call verification, and repeatable replay. Strict-canonical cases must reject
coercions and stale derived values rather than silently repair them. Privacy checks recursively
inspect the complete typed output and manifest. Withheld consent must reject before hostile chain
or artifact traversal. Recovery metadata must name governed correction-and-replay without
rewriting prior evidence. The maximum accepted shape must remain within the declared practical
latency tripwire; the first excess must reject before archive construction.

These are deterministic fixture assertions, not estimates of signature security, attack
resistance, signer authenticity, provenance truth, scientific reproducibility, assay performance,
biological preservation, supply-chain integrity, transportability, or clinical utility. Recovery
means correcting the authoritative chain result, declaration, artifact, statement, policy, or
caller control and producing a new immutable package. Signature override, release exception,
claim promotion, critical discrepancy, novel/OOD state, and unresolved biological conflict
require governed human review outside M02-08.

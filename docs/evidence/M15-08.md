## Locked safety and reconstruction controls

- All seven controls (approved configuration, identity lineage, provenance,
  consent, quality, support, intended use) are checked before source/config
  traversal.
- Source, upstream, manifest, and evidence references remain opaque; no source
  bytes are read, interpreted, or silently rewritten.
- Each chain link has an explicit predecessor and evidence reference. Counter-
  evidence challenges the mechanism link, and a planned orthogonal validation
  route records required experiment and acceptance criterion.
- Measurement, sampling, parameter, model-form, identification, support, and
  transport uncertainty remain visible as not-estimable at this boundary.
- The claim ceiling prohibits KINOPHOS kinase state, generic all-omics fusion,
  direct treatment recommendation, identity/consent inference, upstream
  relabeling, disagreement erasure, and unsupported-to-negative conversion.
- The runtime marks owner review required and never promotes planned validation
  or a caller assertion to a biological conclusion.

## Locked risk controls

- The seven authorization controls are evaluated before request or fact-ledger traversal. A denied
  context cannot be masked by hostile mapping behavior, malformed nested content, an oversized
  graph, or a forged derived digest.
- Executable evidence reconstructs genuine M01-02, M03-01, M03-02, and M03-03 results through
  their public operations. Exact upstream request/result/receipt digests, protocol and search-space
  identities, lineage claims, source summaries, diagnostics, disposition, and parent target are
  revalidated. Handwritten upstream envelopes are forbidden in this harness. Standalone M03-04
  accepts a caller-declared, self-content-addressed compact projection and does not independently
  attest that M03-03 executed or authenticate a signer.
- The fact ledger is content-addressed and closes over complete M03-03 source and claim
  projections through canonical binding digests. This aggregate evidence-graph view binds every
  governed source, lineage claim, metric, control, and provenance relation without a digest cycle.
  A receipt or ledger-binding mismatch fails before metric success.
- All eight metrics are computed from bounded integer counts on the 1,000,000 rate scale.
  Numerators, denominators, reduced values, threshold directions and values, statuses, findings,
  and final disposition are derived rather than trusted. Equal, adjacent, first-excess, and
  zero-denominator boundaries execute.
- Shared evidence and competing explanations are not double-counted or resolved into biological
  truth. Ambiguity burden measures the declared graph only. Censored, missing, not-applicable,
  unsupported, and not-evaluable states remain distinct; no state becomes zero or absence.
- Exactly one assay profile must match protocol, controlled vocabulary, unit system, search space,
  control-group, and sample-context bindings. No match abstains as unsupported; multiple matches
  are a policy error. Labels do not override digests or versions.
- Genuine M03-03 rejected, quarantined, and abstained handoffs propagate before ledger traversal.
  Deterministic precedence is authorization, strict reconstruction, upstream disposition,
  receipt/ledger closure, profile support, metric evaluation, and threshold reduction.
- Strict ingress rejects duplicate JSON keys, scalar coercion, non-finite values, unknown fields or
  terms, silent defaults, stale derived values, collection excess, and the first byte past the
  canonical cap. Installed maximum and first-excess boundaries execute for every governed cap.
- Semantic ordering cannot change the complete result. Typed, dictionary, and strict-JSON
  requests agree. Canonical request, policy, profile, threshold, receipt, ledger, finding,
  and result digests are stable.
- Every nested receipt, projected source/claim fact, metric, threshold, finding, disposition, and
  digest is recomputed for self-consistency. Re-signing an outer object cannot mask an inner
  contradiction, stale closure, arithmetic forgery, threshold promotion, or disposition promotion;
  coherently re-authored caller declarations are not execution-authenticity evidence.
- Library, engine, service, plugin, API, and CLI computation agree. API and CLI schema export must
  return exact installed contracts with M03-04 URNs.
- Recursive ownership/privacy checks exclude direct identifiers, raw identity tokens, peptide
  sequences, accessions, protein or proteoform assertions, abundance, complex-activity or kinase
  scores, subtype, proteotype, fused interpretation, treatment advice, and clinical decisions.
- Recovery is append-only: corrected facts, policies, thresholds, profiles, or upstream handoffs
  produce a new result with explicit supersession provenance; prior evidence is not mutated.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The locked corpus contains exactly eight groups and 56 unique case identifiers, allocated
7/9/7/7/7/7/6/6. Every named case must execute and no parameterized oracle may silently omit a
declared case. A canonical supported workload must qualify reproducibly, emit exactly eight
  metrics, close every source/claim/receipt/ledger binding, preserve the `complex_activity` parent ceiling,
and remain completely equal after semantic reordering and across public surfaces.

All examples are small, synthetic, and non-clinical. Identifiers are opaque and facts are bounded
counts or categorical metadata states. No example contains an observed peptide sequence, real
accession assignment, patient fact, raw abundance, activity score, or treatment information.
Qualification proves deterministic software behavior within the declared profile only. External
authorities, assay truth, laboratory custody, upstream assertions, reference suitability, and
clinical relevance remain governed elsewhere.

Recovery requires correction of the governed fact or policy, execution of a new immutable request,
and explicit supersession provenance. Threshold exceptions, unsupported profiles, unresolved
ambiguity, critical disagreement, novel use, result promotion, or clinical interpretation require
external human review.

## Locked safety controls

- Strict Pydantic models forbid unknown fields, coercion, mutation, NaN and infinity.
- Numeric bounds apply to scalar, interval and vector representations; value kind and unit must
  match the formal schema.
- Missing, not-applicable, unknown and unsupported states cannot carry a value representation and
  never become zero, negative or supported evidence.
- An invariant outside the provisional expression grammar is not evaluable and causes abstention.
- Seven controls are recorded in provenance, with consent granted, identity resolved and five
  upstream decisions accepted before execution.
- The module emits no parent proteotype object and never takes kinase-state ownership.

## Review and recovery

The ABI, feature catalogue, expression grammar, capacities, operation and media type are not
frozen by the dossier. A governed owner review is required before promotion. Evidence and result
digests are immutable; a corrected request must supersede the earlier result and retain its audit
trail.

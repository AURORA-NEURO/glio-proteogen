# M03-05 evidence inventory

This proportionate G1 package verifies deterministic reduction of synthetic protein-inference
artifact evidence. It checks genuine upstream closure, exact integer evidence fractions, threshold
boundaries, signal/unit applicability, missingness fidelity, categorical reduction, contamination
filtering, mask construction, typed safe failure, strict ingress, canonical equality, forgery
resistance, and interface parity. It does not prove contamination, estimate a calibrated
probability, establish laboratory execution, infer a peptide or protein, calculate complex
activity, or qualify biological or clinical performance.

## Locked risk controls

- The seven authorization controls are evaluated before request or artifact-ledger traversal. A
  denied context cannot be masked by hostile mapping behavior, malformed nested content, an
  oversized graph, or a forged derived digest.
- Executable evidence reconstructs genuine M01-02, M03-01, M03-02, M03-03, and M03-04 results
  through public operations. The exact M03-04 result, request, policy, configuration, quality
  receipt, source/claim projections, eight metrics, disposition, support, and parent target are
  revalidated before the harness constructs artifact evidence. Standalone M03-05 accepts a
  caller-declared, self-content-addressed compact projection; it does not independently attest that
  M03-04 executed, authenticate a signer, or prove external reference truth.
- The artifact ledger closes over the quality-result digest, M03-03 admission and source-manifest
  digests, source/claim/quality-metric binding digests, applicability, evidence units, evidence
  artifact, and recorded time. Every unit identifier is unique and every source and claim binding
  must be present and role-compatible with the unit kind.
- Every unit carries all eight signals. A locked applicability matrix decides which unit kinds can
  evaluate each signal. Out-of-domain signals are exactly not applicable and cannot carry counts;
  in-domain signals cannot be mislabeled not applicable.
- Observed signals use exact bounded integer supporting/evaluated fractions on the 1,000,000
  scale, with half-up rounding. Review and exclusion decisions execute below, equal, adjacent,
  first-excess, zero-denominator, and maximum boundaries. No caller rate or floating-point value is
  trusted.
- Evidence scores are deliberately not calibrated probabilities. No classifier, fitted parameter,
  likelihood, posterior distribution, confidence interval, or population performance estimate is
  produced. The required `artifact_posteriors` object is a categorical state reduction and every
  score-bearing output fixes `score_is_calibrated_probability` to false.
- Missing, unsupported, indeterminate, and not-applicable evidence remain distinct. A required
  signal in one of those states cannot be treated as clear or negative. Detected outranks
  suspected, which outranks indeterminate, which outranks clear; quarantine outranks a coexisting
  abstention.
- Contamination flags are restricted to suspected or detected contaminant-reference, barcode/index
  collision, and technical-carryover signals. Non-contamination signals and indeterminate
  contamination evidence cannot be promoted into contamination flags.
- The exclusion mask is a complete disjoint partition of successfully evaluated unit identifiers:
  detected is excluded, suspected or indeterminate is reviewed, and only fully clear required
  evidence is retained.
- Genuine or self-consistent M03-04 rejected, quarantined, and abstained states propagate before
  ledger traversal. Unsupported shape, binding mismatch, profile mismatch, and incompatible unit
  bindings produce typed failures with no successful artifact output.
- Exactly one pairwise-disjoint detector profile must match applicability plus assay-protocol,
  controlled-vocabulary, and unit-system versions. Labels do not override governed content or
  version disagreement.
- Strict ingress rejects duplicate JSON keys, scalar coercion, non-finite values, unknown fields or
  terms, silent defaults, stale derived values, collection excess, and the first byte past the
  canonical cap. Installed maximum and first-excess boundaries execute for every governed cap.
- The result evidence index has an exact reachable ceiling of 18 references: seven authorization
  controls, the policy, the active profile, eight thresholds, and the artifact ledger. The exact
  ceiling and first excess are executable; inactive profiles cannot inflate the result index.
- The exact 64-source, 48-claim, 512-unit result contains 4,096 scores and 1,536 contamination
  flags, remains within the declared 8 MiB result ceiling, and does not reuse the 4 MiB public
  request parser. Its exact mixed-state finding ceiling is 35.
- Semantic ordering cannot change the complete result. Typed, dictionary, and strict-JSON
  requests agree. Canonical request, policy, profile, threshold, receipt, ledger, finding, and
  result digests are stable.
- Every nested projection, binding, score, flag, posterior, mask, finding, disposition, and digest
  is replayed for self-consistency. Re-signing an outer object cannot mask a contradictory inner
  score, threshold promotion, mask promotion, or stale binding; coherently re-authored caller
  declarations remain declaration evidence, not execution-authenticity evidence.
- Library, engine, service, plugin, API, and CLI detection agree. API and CLI schema export return
  exact installed contracts with M03-05 URNs. API and CLI replay verification parse the bounded
  result envelope through the same duplicate-safe strict JSON path and reject a re-signed result
  whose nested score, mask, finding, or result digest no longer closes.
- Recursive ownership/privacy checks exclude direct identifiers, raw identity tokens, peptide
  sequences, accessions, protein or proteoform assertions, abundance, complex-activity or kinase
  scores, subtype, proteotype, fused interpretation, treatment advice, and clinical decisions.
- Recovery is append-only: corrected evidence, policies, thresholds, profiles, or upstream quality
  produce a new result with explicit supersession provenance; prior evidence is not mutated.

## Preregistered synthetic criteria

The locked corpus contains exactly eight groups and 57 unique case identifiers, allocated
7/9/7/7/7/7/6/6. Every named case must execute and no parameterized oracle may silently omit a
declared case. The seeded panel must detect each of the eight locked critical signal classes when
its exact evidence fraction reaches the exclusion threshold. Canonical clean units must have zero
false exclusions. A canonical supported workload must clear reproducibly, close every receipt,
ledger, source, claim, metric, profile, and threshold binding, preserve the `complex_activity`
parent ceiling, and remain completely equal after semantic reordering and across public surfaces.

All examples are small, synthetic, and non-clinical. Projected source, claim, and unit identifiers
are namespaced content-derived 64-hex aliases, and facts are bounded counts or categorical metadata
states. No example contains an observed peptide sequence, real
accession assignment, patient fact, raw abundance, activity score, or treatment information. The
8-of-8 seeded result and zero clean exclusions prove only deterministic behavior on this finite
preregistered corpus. External authorities, laboratory execution, assay sensitivity, contamination
truth, reference suitability, population error, calibration, transportability, and clinical
relevance remain governed elsewhere.

Recovery requires correction of governed evidence or policy, execution of a new immutable request,
and explicit supersession provenance. Threshold exceptions, unsupported profiles, unresolved
artifact or contamination evidence, critical disagreement, novel use, result promotion, or
clinical interpretation require external human review.

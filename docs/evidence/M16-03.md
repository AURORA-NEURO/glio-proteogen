## Locked safety and ownership controls

- All seven controls (approved configuration, identity lineage, provenance,
  consent, quality, support, intended use) are checked before contribution or
  propagation traversal.
- Contributions carry source kind, artifact, claim, reliability score/band,
  uncertainty note, and evidence; source identity is never replaced by an
  inferred label.
- Open disagreements remain explicit. Signed propagation digests remain
  caller-declared and require external signature authority; this runtime does
  not authenticate them.
- Reliability below threshold and non-evaluable sources abstain with review-
  required support; no unsupported state is converted to a negative finding.
- Measurement, sampling, parameter, model-form, identification, support, and
  transport uncertainty remain visible as not-estimable at this boundary.
- KINOPHOS kinase activity, generic all-omics fusion, treatment recommendation,
  identity/consent inference, upstream mutation, and disagreement erasure are
  outside the output ceiling.

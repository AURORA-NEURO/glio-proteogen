## Locked risk controls

- Authorization is fail-closed and precedes upstream traversal. Broken context objects, arbitrary
  mappings and ordinary accessor failures cannot bypass the seven-control gate.
- Unsupported, missing, conflicted and abstained evidence remains explicitly typed and review
  visible. No branch maps absence to a negative biological finding.
- Source artifact IDs and digests are unique and bound to the aligned M19-04 bundle. Nested
  evidence/provenance/action references cannot introduce an unbound artifact or digest.
- The workspace has exactly six required view kinds, contiguous positions and deterministic safe
  ordering. Duplicate or missing views, reordered positions and policy over-capacity reject.
- Result IDs and digests are content-bound. Request, workspace, status, support, provenance and
  source-bundle tampering fails strict reconstruction or replay verification.
- FastAPI, Typer and plugin surfaces perform strict parse-once validation and expose sanitized
  errors. Existing output paths and invalid tokens are rejected without publication.

## Reviewer sign-off and rollback

Local evidence is an engineering gate, not release approval. External review must bind the exact
authority SHA/slice, source commit, schema and fixture digests, evaluator/benchmark reports,
package hashes, consent/provenance/support records and any claim-promotion or exception. On a
critical failure, retain the immutable result, correct the external source, publish a new result
path, and replay all seven scenarios plus interfaces, coverage, package and authority checks.

## Safety and claims ceiling

- The only emitted parent target is the typed `complex_activity` object; `emits_parent` is false.
- The operation emits no kinase activity, generic all-omics fusion, treatment recommendation,
  raw spectra, peptide/accession/sequence content, identity inference, consent inference, or
  upstream mutation.
- Missing candidates, unsupported scopes, low support, OOD candidates, coverage outside 85%-95%,
  calibration error above its configured ceiling, and subgroup disparity above its configured
  ceiling cannot become a negative finding. They return a typed abstention with explicit support
  status and review state.
- Candidate metrics are caller-declared and not issuer-authenticated. A calibrated status is only
  the deterministic result of the declared gates; this package proves contract behavior, not model
  validity, calibration validity, transportability, biological truth, or clinical utility.
- Seven control references are checked before candidate traversal. Consent and identity are never
  inferred locally.

## Recovery

Results are immutable and content-addressed. Corrections must be submitted as a new request with
`supersedes_result_digest`; no prior request/result/configuration is overwritten, relabeled, or
silently promoted. Critical discrepancy, OOD state, support override, claim promotion, release
exception, and unresolved biological conflict remain subject to external human review.

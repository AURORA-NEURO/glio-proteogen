caller-declared metadata. It retains source attribution, view status, uncertainty, discrepancies,
provenance, safe ordering, automation-bias warnings, explicit abstention, canonical replay, and
tamper detection. It does not prove biological truth, calibration, transportability, identity,
consent validity, clinical utility, or usability acceptance.

## Safety controls

- Seven caller-declared controls are inspected before review material is traversed; denial fails
  closed.
- The exact M17-02 aligned-evidence media type is required and is never relabeled or replaced.
- Review item evidence, uncertainty summaries, discrepancies, status, and provenance remain
  attributable; no conflict is erased or converted into a negative conclusion.
- Unsupported or unresolved items produce safe abstention without a workspace; limited and
  conflicted items remain visible with review-required support and explicit findings.
- The parent target is recorded but never emitted; kinase, generic all-omics, treatment, identity,
  and consent inference remain outside the output ceiling.

The ABI is provisional and cannot self-approve. A governed release must review exact source,
fixture, evaluator, benchmark, coverage, package, authority, and critical-finding evidence.
